### Intro

> _“The mind, brahmin, takes recourse in mindfulness (sati).”_

> _“And sir Gotama, what is it that mindfulness (sati) takes recourse in?”_

> _“Mindfulness (sati), brahmin, takes recourse in liberation.”_

> _“And sir Gotama, what is it that liberation takes recourse in?”_

> _“Liberation, brahmin, takes recourse in Nibbāna.”_

> — [SN 48.42 ¶6 - ¶10](/sn48.42#6-10)
