“Pañcime, bhikkhave, dāne ānisaṁsā. Katame pañca?

Bahuno janassa piyo hoti manāpo; santo sappurisā bhajanti; kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggacchati; gihidhammā anapagato hoti; kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dāne ānisaṁsāti.

Dadamāno piyo hoti,
sataṁ dhammaṁ anukkamaṁ;
Santo naṁ sadā bhajanti,
saññatā brahmacārayo.

Te tassa dhammaṁ desenti,
Sabbadukkhāpanūdanaṁ;
Yaṁ so dhammaṁ idhaññāya,
Parinibbāti anāsavo”ti.
