---
title: Macchariya sutta - Stinginess
description: The Buddha lists the seven fetters that bind beings.
qualities: aversion, doubt, conceit, jealousy, stinginess
theme: principle
slug: an7.10
---

“Sattimāni, bhikkhave, saṁyojanāni. Katamāni satta?

Anunayasaṁyojanaṁ, paṭighasaṁyojanaṁ, diṭṭhisaṁyojanaṁ, vicikicchāsaṁyojanaṁ, mānasaṁyojanaṁ, issāsaṁyojanaṁ, macchariyasaṁyojanaṁ.

Imāni kho, bhikkhave, satta saṁyojanānī”ti.
