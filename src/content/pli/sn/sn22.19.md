Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

“Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo rūpassa uppādāya, sopi dukkho. Dukkhasambhūtaṁ, bhikkhave, rūpaṁ kuto sukhaṁ bhavissati.

Vedanā dukkhā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo vedanāya uppādāya, sopi dukkho. Dukkhasambhūtā, bhikkhave, vedanā kuto sukhā bhavissati.

Saññā dukkhā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo saññāya uppādāya, sopi dukkho. Dukkhasambhūtā, bhikkhave, saññā kuto sukhā bhavissati.

saṅkhārā dukkhā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo saṅkhārānaṁ uppādāya, sopi dukkho. Dukkhasambhūtā, bhikkhave, saṅkhārā kuto sukhā bhavissanti.

Viññāṇaṁ dukkhaṁ. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo viññāṇassa uppādāya, sopi dukkho. Dukkhasambhūtaṁ, bhikkhave, viññāṇaṁ kuto sukhaṁ bhavissati.

Evaṁ passaṁ, bhikkhave, sutavā ariyasāvako rūpasmimpi nibbindati, vedanāyapi nibbindati, saññāyapi nibbindati, saṅkhāresupi nibbindati, viññāṇasmimpi nibbindati. Nibbindaṁ virajjati; virāgā vimuccati. Vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti.

‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā’ti pajānātī”ti.
