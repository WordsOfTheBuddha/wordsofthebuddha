---
title: Sahetuanatta sutta - With A Not-self Cause
description: The causes, supporting conditions for the arising of the five aggregates are not-self, so then how could the five aggregates be self?
qualities: form, feeling, perception, intentional constructs, consciousness, disenchantment, recognition of not-self

Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

“Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, anattā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo rūpassa uppādāya, sopi anattā. Anattasambhūtaṁ, bhikkhave, rūpaṁ kuto attā bhavissati.

Vedanā anattā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo vedanāya uppādāya, sopi anattā. Anattasambhūtā, bhikkhave, vedanā kuto attā bhavissati.

Saññā anattā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo saññāya uppādāya, sopi anattā. Anattasambhūtā, bhikkhave, saññā kuto attā bhavissati.

saṅkhārā anattā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo saṅkhārānaṁ uppādāya, sopi anattā. Anattasambhūtā, bhikkhave, saṅkhārā kuto attā bhavissanti.

Viññāṇaṁ anattā. Yopi hetu yopi paccayo viññāṇassa uppādāya, sopi anattā. Anattasambhūtaṁ, bhikkhave, viññāṇaṁ kuto attā bhavissati.

Evaṁ passaṁ, bhikkhave, sutavā ariyasāvako rūpasmimpi nibbindati, vedanāyapi nibbindati, saññāyapi nibbindati, saṅkhāresupi nibbindati, viññāṇasmimpi nibbindati. Nibbindaṁ virajjati; virāgā vimuccati. Vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti.

‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā’ti pajānātī”ti.
