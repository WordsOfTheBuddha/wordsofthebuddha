## Right Understanding

Right understanding knows suffering, its origin, its extinction, and the path, and knows which actions are wholesome. The rest of this chapter turns that understanding toward views of a self: the questions that lead nowhere, the views that bind, and the fetters that stream-entry leaves behind.
