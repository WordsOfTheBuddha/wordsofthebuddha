This was said by the Blessed One, said by the Awakened One, as I have heard:

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate with other beings |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. Those |of inferior inclinations::with inferior dispositions, with low tendencies [hīnādhimuttikā]| come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations; those |of noble inclinations::with ethical inclinations [kalyāṇādhimuttika]| come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.

In the past too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings came together and associated with other beings by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations came together and associated with those of inferior inclinations; those of noble inclinations came together and associated with those of noble inclinations.

In the future too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings will come together and associate with other beings by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations will come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations; those of noble inclinations will come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.

Even now, |bhikkhus,::::| at the present time, beings come together and associate with other beings by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations; those of noble inclinations come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.”

The Blessed One spoke on this matter. In this regard, it is said:

“From |association::mingling, mixing, contact, intimacy, touch, entanglement [saṃsagga]|, |craving::entanglements, subtle attachments, desire [vanatha]| arises,
through non-association, it |is cut off::is severed, ceases to exist, is broken [chijjati]|;
like one who boards a fragile raft,
and sinks in the mighty ocean.

So by consorting with a |lazy person::a procrastinating, inactive person, indolent [kusīta]|,
even a virtuous person sinks;
therefore one should |steer clear of::avoid, shun [parivajjati]| a lazy person,
who is |weak in effort::low in energy, lacking in endurance [hīnavīriya]|.

One should |dwell together::keep company [sahāvasa]| with the wise,
with the |secluded::detached, separated [pavivitta]| |Noble Ones::distinguished, an ethically noble person, an awakened being [ariya]|;
with |resolute::determined, intent [pahitatta]| |meditators::yogis [jhāyi]|,
who are |continuously::regularly, reliably [nicca]| |energetic::with energy aroused, with initiative [āraddhavīriya]|.”

This matter too was spoken by the Blessed One, as I have heard.
