Then the |Blessed One::Sublime One, Fortunate One, epithet of the Buddha [bhagavā]| addressed those bhikkhus: “Bhikkhus, I am freed from all snares, those that are divine and those that are human. You too, bhikkhus, are freed from all snares, those that are divine and those that are human. Wander, bhikkhus, for the welfare of the many, for the happiness of the many, out of compassion for the world, for the benefit, welfare, and happiness of gods and humans. Do not go two together. Teach the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]|, bhikkhus, that is good in the beginning, good in the middle, and good in the end. Proclaim the |spiritual life::a life of celibacy, contemplation, and ethical discipline lived for the sake of liberation [brahmacariya]| with its meaning and its phrasing, complete and pure. There are beings with little dust in their eyes who are suffering loss from not hearing the Dhamma. There will be those who understand the Dhamma. I too, bhikkhus, will go to |Uruvelā::name of a town in Magadha, lit. broad banks [uruvelā]|, to Senānigama, to teach the Dhamma.”

Then |Māra::the ruler of the sensual realm, often depicted as a tempter who tries to obstruct beings from the path to liberation [māra]| the Evil One approached the Blessed One and addressed him in verse:
“You are bound by all the snares,
those that are divine and those that are human.
You are bound by a great bond.
You will not escape me, ascetic.”
“I am freed from all the snares,
those that are divine and those that are human.
I am freed from the great bond.
You are defeated, |End-maker::Māra, the one who brings beings to their end [antaka]|.”
“There is a snare moving in the sky,
the one that travels in the mind.
With that I will bind you.
You will not escape me, ascetic.”
“Forms, sounds, tastes, and smells,
and touches that delight the mind —
desire for these has left me.
You are defeated, End-maker.”
Then Māra the Evil One, thinking, “The Blessed One knows me. The Accomplished One knows me,” miserable and dejected, vanished right there.
