Then the |Blessed One::Sublime One, Fortunate One, epithet of the Buddha [bhagavā]|, having spent the rains, addressed the bhikkhus: “Bhikkhus, it is through |radical attention::wise steering of attention, root-level attention, attention to the structural source, contextual reflection; lit. attention from the source [yoniso + manasikāra]| and radically striving aright that I have attained the unsurpassed liberation and |personally experienced::realized for oneself [sacchikata]| the unsurpassed liberation. You too, bhikkhus, should attain the unsurpassed liberation and personally experience the unsurpassed liberation through radical attention and right striving.”

Then |Māra::the ruler of the sensual realm, often depicted as a tempter who tries to obstruct beings from the path to liberation [māra]| the Evil One approached the Blessed One and addressed him in verse:
“You are bound by Māra’s snares,
those that are divine and those that are human.
You are bound by a great bond.
You will not escape me, ascetic.”
“I am freed from Māra’s snares,
those that are divine and those that are human.
I am freed from the great bond.
You are defeated, |End-maker::Māra, the one who brings beings to their end [antaka]|.”
Then Māra the Evil One, thinking, “The Blessed One knows me. The Accomplished One knows me,” miserable and dejected, vanished right there.
