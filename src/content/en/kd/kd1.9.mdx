description: Bhikkhus are tiring as they bring candidates from every region for the Blessed One to ordain. He allows them to give the going forth and full ordination themselves, by three goings for refuge.

At that time bhikkhus were bringing from various directions and various countries those who wanted the going forth and those who wanted full ordination, thinking: “The |Blessed One::Sublime One, Fortunate One, epithet of the Buddha [bhagavā]| will give them the going forth and full ordination.” In that, the bhikkhus were tiring, and those who wanted the going forth and those who wanted full ordination were tiring too. Then, while the Blessed One was alone in seclusion, this reflection arose in his mind: “Now the bhikkhus are bringing from various directions and various countries those who want the going forth and those who want full ordination, thinking: ‘The Blessed One will give them the going forth and full ordination.’ In that, the bhikkhus are tiring, and those who want the going forth and those who want full ordination are tiring too. Suppose I were to allow the bhikkhus: ‘You yourselves, bhikkhus, may now give the going forth and full ordination in those various directions and those various countries.’”

Then the Blessed One, emerging from seclusion in the evening, addressed the bhikkhus with a talk on the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]| concerning this occasion and this incident: “Here, bhikkhus, while I was alone in seclusion, this reflection arose in my mind: ‘Now the bhikkhus are bringing from various directions and various countries those who want the going forth and those who want full ordination, thinking: “The Blessed One will give them the going forth and full ordination.” In that, the bhikkhus are tiring, and those who want the going forth and those who want full ordination are tiring too. Suppose I were to allow the bhikkhus: “You yourselves, bhikkhus, may now give the going forth and full ordination in those various directions and those various countries.”’ I allow you, bhikkhus: you yourselves may now give the going forth and full ordination in those various directions and those various countries.

“And, bhikkhus, one should be given the going forth and full ordination in this way. First, have him shave off his hair and beard, put on the dyed robes, arrange the upper robe over one shoulder, pay homage at the bhikkhus’ feet, squat down, raise his joined palms, and tell him: ‘Say this: “I go for refuge to the Buddha. I go for refuge to the Dhamma. I go for refuge to the |Saṅgha::the community of disciples practicing in line with the Buddha’s teachings [saṅgha]|. A second time I go for refuge to the Buddha. A second time I go for refuge to the Dhamma. A second time I go for refuge to the Saṅgha. A third time I go for refuge to the Buddha. A third time I go for refuge to the Dhamma. A third time I go for refuge to the Saṅgha.”’ I allow, bhikkhus, the going forth and full ordination by these three goings for refuge.”
