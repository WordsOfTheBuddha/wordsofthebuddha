Then the |Blessed One::Sublime One, Fortunate One, epithet of the Buddha [bhagavā]|, having stayed at |Uruvelā::name of a town in Magadha, lit. broad banks [uruvelā]| as long as he wished, set out for |Gayāsīsa::Gayā’s Head, a hill near Gayā [gayāsīsa]| with a great |Saṅgha::the community of disciples practicing in line with the Buddha’s teachings [saṅgha]| of bhikkhus, a thousand bhikkhus, all of them former matted-hair ascetics. There the Blessed One stayed at Gayā, on Gayā’s Head, together with the thousand bhikkhus.

<p class="not-prose my-6 rounded-md border px-4 py-3 text-sm leading-relaxed" style="border-color:var(--surface-border);background:var(--surface-elevated);">The discourse spoken here is <a href="/sn35.28">SN 35.28 — The Fire Sermon</a>.</p>

And while this exposition was being spoken, the minds of that thousand bhikkhus were liberated from the taints through non-clinging.
