At one time, the Blessed One was traveling on the highway between |Madhurā::name of a town; capital of Surasena [madhurā]| and Verañjā. A number of householders and their wives were also traveling on the highway between Madhurā and Verañjā. Then the Blessed One stepped off the path and sat down at the foot of a certain tree. Those householders and their wives saw the Blessed One sitting at the foot of a certain tree.

Seeing him, they went to the Blessed One. Having approached, they paid homage to the Blessed One and sat down to one side. When they were seated to one side, the Blessed One said to those householders and their wives:

“Householders, there are these four ways of living together. What four?

A |wretch::vile person; lit. a corpse [chava]| lives together with a wretch;

a wretch lives together with a goddess;

a god lives together with a wretch;

a god lives together with a goddess.

And how, householders, does a wretch live together with a wretch? Here, householders, the husband |kills living beings::destroys life [pāṇātipātī]|, |takes what is not given::steals [adinnādāyī]|, |engages in sexual misconduct::is adulterous [kāmesumicchācārī]|, |speaks falsely::lies [musāvādī]|, and consumes wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; he is |unprincipled::without regard for ethical conduct [dussīla]|, |endowed with harmful qualities::one who has had a long association with harmful mental qualities [pāpadhamma]|, dwells at home with a mind obsessed by the stain of stinginess, and insults and reviles ascetics and brahmins. And his wife also kills living beings, takes what is not given, engages in sexual misconduct, speaks falsely, and consumes wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; she is unprincipled, endowed with harmful qualities, dwells at home with a mind obsessed by the stain of stinginess, and insults and reviles ascetics and brahmins. In this way, householders, a wretch lives together with a wretch.

And how, householders, does a wretch live together with a goddess? Here, householders, the husband kills living beings, takes what is not given, engages in sexual misconduct, speaks falsely, and consumes wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; he is unprincipled, endowed with harmful qualities, dwells at home with a mind obsessed by the stain of stinginess, and insults and reviles ascetics and brahmins. But his wife refrains from killing living beings, refrains from taking what is not given, refrains from sexual misconduct, refrains from false speech, and refrains from consuming wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; she is |virtuous::ethical, moral [sīlavant]|, |endowed with wholesome qualities::one who has had a long association with wholesome mental qualities [kalyāṇadhamma]|, dwells at home with a mind free from the stain of stinginess, and does not insult or revile ascetics and brahmins. In this way, householders, a wretch lives together with a goddess.

And how, householders, does a god live together with a wretch? Here, householders, the husband refrains from killing living beings, refrains from taking what is not given, refrains from sexual misconduct, refrains from false speech, and refrains from consuming wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; he is virtuous, endowed with wholesome qualities, dwells at home with a mind free from the stain of stinginess, and does not insult or revile ascetics and brahmins. But his wife kills living beings<collapse>, takes what is not given, engages in sexual misconduct, speaks falsely,</collapse> and consumes wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; she is unprincipled, endowed with harmful qualities, dwells at home with a mind obsessed by the stain of stinginess, and insults and reviles ascetics and brahmins. In this way, householders, a god lives together with a wretch.

And how, householders, does a god live together with a goddess? Here, householders, the husband refrains from killing living beings<collapse>, refrains from taking what is not given, refrains from sexual misconduct, refrains from false speech,</collapse> and refrains from consuming wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; he is virtuous, endowed with wholesome qualities, dwells at home with a mind free from the stain of stinginess, and does not insult or revile ascetics and brahmins. And his wife also refrains from killing living beings<collapse>, refrains from taking what is not given, refrains from sexual misconduct, refrains from false speech,</collapse> and refrains from consuming wine, liquor, and intoxicants that are the basis for negligence; she is virtuous, endowed with wholesome qualities, dwells at home with a mind free from the stain of stinginess, and does not insult or revile ascetics and brahmins. In this way, householders, a god lives together with a goddess.

These, householders, are the four ways of living together.”

“When both are unprincipled,
miserly and abusive,
husband and wife
live together as wretches.

The husband is unprincipled,
miserly and abusive;
but his wife is virtuous,
generous, free from stinginess.
She, a goddess, lives together
with a wretched husband.

The husband is virtuous,
generous, free from stinginess;
but his wife is unprincipled,
miserly and abusive.
She, a wretch, lives together
with a godly husband.

Both are faithful and generous,
restrained, living in accordance with the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]|;
husband and wife [live together]
speaking kindly to one another.

Then plentiful benefits arise for them,
and they dwell in ease;
their enemies are dejected,
when both are equal in ethical conduct.

Having lived by the Dhamma here,
both with the same precepts and practices;
they delight in a heavenly world,
pleased, enjoying sensual pleasures.”
