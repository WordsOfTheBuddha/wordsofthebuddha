---
title: Pañcamacchariya sutta - Five Kinds of Stinginess
description: Stinginess regarding the Dhamma is the worst of the five kinds of stinginess.
qualities: stinginess
theme: wisdom
slug: an5.254
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are five kinds of stinginess. What five? 1.) |Stinginess::selfishness, meanness, tight-fistedness [macchariya]| with regard to |dwelling place::residence, home [āvāsa]|, 2.) stinginess with regard to family, 3.) stinginess with regard to |acquisitions::gain, money, profit, possessions [lābha]|, 4.) stinginess with regard to |praise::approval [vaṇṇa]|, and 5.) stinginess with regard to the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five kinds of stinginess. The worst of these five kinds of stinginess is stinginess with regard to the Dhamma.”
