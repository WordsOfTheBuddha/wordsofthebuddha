---
title: Paṭhama sammattaniyāma sutta - The Fixed Course of Rightness (First)
description: The Buddha explains that to enter upon the fixed course of rightness, one must not disparage the teaching, speaker, or oneself, but listen with a settled mind while paying radical attention.
qualities: radical attention, superficial attention, respect, humility, wholesome, unification
theme: cultivating discernment, wisdom
slug: an5.151
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| possessing five qualities, even when listening to the |good Dhamma::good teaching, true doctrine [saddhamma]|, one is incapable of entering upon the fixed course of rightness in |wholesome qualities::skillful actions, good habits [kusaladhammā]|. What five?

1.) One |disparages::disrespects [paribhoti]| the |talk::conversation, discussion [kathā]|; 2.) one disparages the speaker; 3.) one disparages oneself; 4.) one listens to the Dhamma with a distracted and scattered mind; 5.) one attends to it |superficially::carelessly, unwisely; lit. not according to the source [ayoniso]|. Possessing these five qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| even when listening to the good Dhamma, one is incapable of entering upon the fixed course of rightness in wholesome qualities.

|Bhikkhus,::::| possessing five qualities, when listening to the good Dhamma, one is capable of entering upon the fixed course of rightness in |wholesome qualities::skillful actions, good habits [kusaladhammā]|. What five?

1.) One does not disparage the talk; 2.) one does not disparage the speaker; 3.) one does not disparage oneself; 4.) one listens to the Dhamma |with a settled::with an unscattered mind [avikkhittacitta]| and |unified mind::with a one-pointed mind; with an integrated, well-composed, concentrated mind [ekaggacitta]|; 5.) one attends to it |radically::wisely, prudently, thoroughly, carefully; lit. according to the source [yoniso]|. Possessing these five qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| when listening to the good Dhamma, one is capable of entering upon the fixed course of rightness in wholesome qualities.”
