#### 2.130

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a |faithful::confident, believing, devoted, trusting [saddha]| bhikkhu, aspiring rightly, should aspire thus: ‘May I become like |Sāriputta::foremost disciple of the Buddha in great wisdom [sāriputta]| and |Moggallāna::foremost disciple of the Buddha in psychic power; lit. Moggallāna the great [moggallāna]|!’ For|, bhikkhus,::::| this is the measure and standard for my bhikkhu disciples, namely, Sāriputta and Moggallāna.”

#### 2.131

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a faithful bhikkhunī, aspiring rightly, should aspire thus: ‘May I become like the bhikkhunīs |Khemā::foremost bhikkhunī disciple of the Buddha in great wisdom [khemā]| and |Uppalavaṇṇā::foremost bhikkhunī disciple of the Buddha in psychic power [uppalavaṇṇā]|!’ For|, bhikkhus,::::| this is the measure and standard for my bhikkhunī disciples, namely, the bhikkhunīs Khemā and Uppalavaṇṇā.”

#### 2.132

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a faithful layman, aspiring rightly, should aspire thus: ‘May I become like |Citta the householder::foremost lay disciple of the Buddha in giving Dhamma discourses [citta]::Chitta the householder| and |Hatthaka of Āḷavi::foremost lay disciple of the Buddha in maintaining a following through the four means of social cohesion. Read more about him in [AN 8.23 - Paṭhamahatthaka sutta](/an8.23). [hatthaka]|!’ For|, bhikkhus,::::| this is the measure and standard for my male lay disciples, namely, |Citta::::Chitta| the householder and Hatthaka of Āḷavi.”

#### 2.133

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a faithful laywoman, aspiring rightly, should aspire thus: ‘May I become like the |laywoman Khujjuttarā::foremost female lay disciple of the Buddha among those who were very learned [khujjuttarā]| and |Nandamātā from Veḷukaṇḍaka::foremost female lay disciple of the Buddha, read about her in [AN 7.53 - Nandamātā sutta](/an7.53). [veḷukaṇḍakiyā]|!’ For|, bhikkhus,::::| this is the measure and standard for my female lay disciples, namely, the laywoman Khujjuttarā and Nandamātā from Veḷukaṇḍaka.”

#### 2.134

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, an |immature::lacking in discernment or good sense, child-like in understanding [bāla]|, |incompetent::unaccomplished, undistinguished, unskilled [abyatta]| |person lacking in integrity::unethical person, inferior person [asappurisa]| maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the |wise::discerning, intelligent, who can distinguish [viññū]|, and accumulates much |demerit::unwholesome kamma, bad fortune [apuñña]|. What two? Without examining or scrutinizing, they speak praise of one who deserves dispraise; and without examining or scrutinizing, they speak dispraise of one who deserves praise. Endowed with these two qualities|, bhikkhus,::::| an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit.

|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, a |wise::astute, intelligent, learned, skilled [paṇḍita]|, |competent::accomplished, capable, learned [viyatta]| |person of integrity::virtuous person, true person [sappurisa]| maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much |merit::wholesome kamma, good fortune [puñña]|. What two? After examining and scrutinizing, they speak dispraise of one who deserves dispraise; and after examining and scrutinizing, they speak praise of one who deserves praise. Endowed with these two qualities|, bhikkhus,::::| a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit.”

#### 2.135

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit. What two? Without examining or scrutinizing, they manifest |serene confidence::faith, trust, clarity of heart [pasāda]| in what is uninspiring; and without examining or scrutinizing, they manifest |lack of confidence::distrust, disbelief, displeasure [appasāda]| in what is inspiring. Endowed with these two qualities|, bhikkhus,::::| an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit.

|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit. What two? After examining and scrutinizing, they manifest lack of confidence in what is uninspiring; and after examining and scrutinizing, they manifest serene confidence in what is inspiring. Endowed with these two qualities|, bhikkhus,::::| a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit.”

#### 2.136

“|Bhikkhus,::::| acting wrongly toward two individuals, an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit. Toward what two? Toward one’s mother and father. Acting wrongly toward these two individuals|, bhikkhus,::::| an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit.

|Bhikkhus,::::| acting rightly toward two individuals, a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit. Toward what two? Toward one’s mother and father. Acting rightly toward these two individuals|, bhikkhus,::::| a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit.”

#### 2.137

“|Bhikkhus,::::| acting wrongly toward two individuals, an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit. Toward what two? Toward the |Tathāgata::one who has arrived at the truth, an epithet of a perfectly Awakened One [tathāgata]| and a disciple of the Tathāgata. Acting wrongly toward these two individuals|, bhikkhus,::::| an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit.

|Bhikkhus,::::| acting rightly toward two individuals, a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit. Toward what two? Toward the Tathāgata and a disciple of the Tathāgata. Acting rightly toward these two individuals|, bhikkhus,::::| a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit.”

#### 2.138

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Purification of one’s own mind::cleansing, clearing, brightening of one’s own heart/mind [sacittavodāna]| and |not clinging::not grasping, not holding on, not taking up [na upādiyati]| to anything in the world. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.139

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Anger::rage, wrath, fury, indignation [kodha]| and |resentment::bearing a grudge, harboring enmity [upanāha]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.140

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Removing anger::subduing, dispelling, or training away anger [kodhavinaya]| and |removing resentment::subduing, dispelling, or training away resentment [upanāhavinaya]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”
