#### 1.140

“Bhikkhus, those bhikkhus who |explain::illustrate, illuminate [dīpenti]| what is not the Dhamma as not the Dhamma are acting for the welfare of many people, for the happiness of many people, for the benefit, welfare, and happiness of many people, of gods and human beings. These bhikkhus generate much merit and help to establish the |good Dhamma::good teaching, true nature of things [saddhamma]|.”
