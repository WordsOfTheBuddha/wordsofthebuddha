---
title: Mala sutta - Stains
description: One who possesses three qualities without abandoning three stains—immorality, jealousy, and stinginess—is placed in hell just as if carried and set down there, while one who is virtuous, free from jealousy, and generous after abandoning those three stains is placed in heaven.
qualities: unprincipled conduct, ethical conduct, jealousy, stinginess, giving, giving up
theme: principle, cultivating discernment
slug: an3.10
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| someone endowed with three qualities, without having abandoned three |stains::impurities, taints, tarnishes [mala]|, is |placed::dropped, set down [nikkhitta]| in |hell::a place of intense suffering, lit. no good fortune [niraya]|, just as if they were |carried::brought [yathābhata]| there. What are these three? They are |unprincipled::immoral, without regard for ethical conduct [dussīla]|, and the |stain of immorality::tarnish of unprincipled conduct [dussīlyamala]| has not been abandoned in them; they are |jealous::envious, resentful of others’ success [issukī]|, and the |stain of jealousy::tarnish of envy [issāmala]| has not been abandoned in them; they are |stingy::selfish, tight-fisted, possessive [maccharī]|, and the |stain of stinginess::tarnish of selfishness [maccheramala]| has not been abandoned in them.

Endowed with these three qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| without having abandoned these three stains, one is placed in hell, just as if they were carried there.

|Bhikkhus,::::| someone endowed with three qualities, having abandoned three stains, is placed in |heaven::a heavenly realm of happiness and good fortune [sagga]|, just as if they were carried there. What are these three? They are |virtuous::ethical, moral [sīlavant]|, and the stain of immorality has been abandoned in them; they are |free from jealousy::not envious, unresentful of others’ success [anissukī]|, and the stain of jealousy has been abandoned in them; they are |generous::unselfish, open-handed, free from stinginess [amaccharī]|, and the stain of stinginess has been abandoned in them.

Endowed with these three qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| having abandoned these three stains, one is placed in heaven, just as if they were carried there.”
