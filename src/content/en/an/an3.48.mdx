---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| supported by the Himalayas, the king of mountains, great sal trees grow in three ways. What three? They grow in branches, leaves, and foliage; they grow in bark and shoots; and they grow in softwood and |heartwood::essence, core, pith [sāra]|. Supported by the Himalayas, the king of mountains, great sal trees grow in these three ways.

Just so, |bhikkhus,::::| supported by a faithful |head of the family::master of the house, leader of the clan [kulapati]|, the household grows in three ways. What three? They grow in |faith::confidence, conviction, trust [saddhā]|, they grow in |virtue::moral conduct, ethical behavior [sīla]|, and they grow in |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]|. Supported by a faithful head of the family, |bhikkhus,::::| the household grows in these three ways.

Just as the trees
supported by a rocky mountain
in the wilderness of a mighty forest,
grow to become the lords of the forest;

So too, when a head of the family here
is |accomplished in virtue::of excellent morality; accomplished in ethical conduct [sīlasampanna]| and faith,
his wife, children, relatives,
all grow supported by him;
so too his colleagues and circle of relatives,
and those dependent on him for their livelihood.

Those |attentive ones::lit. seeing clearly [vicakkhaṇa]|,
seeing that virtuous man’s good conduct,
his |generosity::relinquishment, giving, liberality [cāga]| and good deeds,
emulate his example for their own benefit.

Having lived here in accordance with the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha; the law of nature [dhamma]|,
the path leading to a |good destination::happy realm, fortunate bourn [sugati]|,
those who desire sensual pleasures rejoice,
delighting in the world of gods.”
