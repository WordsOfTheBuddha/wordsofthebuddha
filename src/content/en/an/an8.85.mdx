“‘Ascetic|,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the |Tathāgata::one who has arrived at the truth, an epithet of a perfectly Awakened One [tathāgata]|, the |Arahant::a worthy one, a fully awakened being, epithet of the Buddha [arahant]|, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘Brahmin|,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘|Master of true knowledge::one who has penetrated spiritual knowledge; lit. knower of the Vedas [vedagū]||,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘|Healer::doctor, physician [bhisakka]||,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘Spotless one|,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘Stainless one|,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘Knower|,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

‘Liberated one|,’ bhikkhus,::::’| is a term for the Tathāgata, the Arahant, the perfectly Awakened One.

That unsurpassed state to be attained by an ascetic,
by a brahmin who has lived the spiritual life;
to be attained by a master of true knowledge
and by the healer—

To be attained by a spotless one,
by a stainless one who is purified;
that unsurpassed state to be attained by the knower
and by the one who is liberated—

I am victorious in battle,
freed, I free others from bondage;
I am a |nāga::noble person, epithet of an Arahant [nāga]|, supremely tamed,
an |adept::arahant; lit. not a trainee, beyond training [asekha]|, |completely quenched::completely cooled of mental defilements [parinibbuta]|.”
