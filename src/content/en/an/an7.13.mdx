---
title: Kula sutta - Family
description: The Buddha shares seven factors that make a family unsuitable to approach or sit with, such as failing to greet pleasantly, concealing wealth, or giving disrespectfully.
qualities: respect, contempt, faith
theme: cultivating discernment, wisdom
slug: an7.13
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a family possessing seven factors that has not yet been approached is not suitable to approach, or one that has been approached is not worth sitting with. What seven? 1.) They do not rise up pleasantly, 2.) they do not bow down pleasantly, 3.) they do not offer a seat pleasantly, 4.) they conceal what they have, 5.) though having much they give little, 6.) though having excellent things they give coarse things, and 7.) they give |inattentively::carelessly, disrespectfully, without faith [asakkaccaṁ]|, not |respectfully::carefully, properly, thoroughly [sakkaccaṁ]|. |Bhikkhus,::::| possessing these seven factors, a family that has not yet been approached is not suitable to approach, or one that has been approached is not worth sitting with.

|Bhikkhus,::::| a family possessing seven factors that has not yet been approached is suitable to approach, or one that has been approached is worth sitting with. What seven? 1.) They rise up pleasantly, 2.) they bow down pleasantly, 3.) they offer a seat pleasantly, 4.) they do not conceal what they have, 5.) though having much they give much, 6.) though having excellent things they give excellent things, and 7.) they give respectfully, not inattentively. |Bhikkhus,::::| possessing these seven factors, a family that has not yet been approached is suitable to approach, or one that has been approached is worth sitting with.”
