#### 1.575

“|Bhikkhus,::::| for anyone who has encompassed the great ocean in their mind, all the streams flowing into it are included. Similarly, |bhikkhus,::::| for anyone who has cultivated and frequently practiced mindfulness of the body, all wholesome qualities that are part of the path to realization are included.”

#### 1.576–582

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, it leads to great urgency, to great benefit, to great safety from bondage, to |radical comprehension::complete comprehension of the arising, persisting, and fading away of phenomena, structural comprehension, discerning mindfulness [satisampajañña]|, to the acquisition of wisdom and vision, to a pleasant abiding in the present life, to the realization of the fruit of true knowledge and liberation. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, leads to great urgency, to great benefit, to great safety from bondage, to radical comprehension, to the acquisition of wisdom and vision, to blissful living in the present life, to the realization of the fruit of true knowledge and liberation.”

#### 1.583

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, both body and mind become tranquil, thought and examination settle down, and all qualities that partake of true knowledge reach full development through cultivation. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, both body and mind become tranquil, thought and examination settle down, and all qualities that partake of true knowledge reach full development through cultivation.”

#### 1.584

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, unarisen unwholesome qualities do not arise, and arisen unwholesome qualities are abandoned. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, unarisen unwholesome qualities do not arise, and arisen unwholesome qualities are abandoned.”

#### 1.585

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, unarisen wholesome qualities arise, and arisen wholesome qualities increase and expand. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, unarisen wholesome qualities arise, and arisen wholesome qualities increase and expand.”

#### 1.586–590

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, ignorance is abandoned, true knowledge arises, the conceit ‘I am’ is abandoned, underlying tendencies are eradicated, fetters are abandoned. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, ignorance is abandoned, true knowledge arises, the conceit ‘I am’ is abandoned, underlying tendencies are eradicated, fetters are abandoned.”

#### 1.591–592

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, it leads to the distinction of wisdom and to final Nibbāna without |clinging::grasping, acquiring, appropriating, taking possession, identifying; fuel for fire; lit. taking near [upādāna]|. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, leads to the distinction of wisdom ... to final Nibbāna without clinging.”

#### 1.593–595

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, there is the penetration of manifold elements, the penetration of diverse elements, and the analytical discrimination of manifold elements. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, there is the penetration of manifold elements, the penetration of diverse elements, the analytical discrimination of manifold elements.”

#### 1.596–599

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, it leads to the realization of the fruit of stream-entry ... the fruit of once-returning ... the fruit of non-returning ... the fruit of arahantship. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, leads to the realization of the fruit of stream-entry ... the fruit of once-returning ... the fruit of non-returning ... the fruit of arahantship.”

#### 1.600–615

“|Bhikkhus,::::| when one quality is cultivated and frequently practiced, it leads to the acquisition of wisdom, to the growth of wisdom, to the expansion of wisdom, to great wisdom, to widespread wisdom, to abundant wisdom, to profound wisdom, to extraordinary wisdom, to vast wisdom, to ample wisdom, to quick wisdom, to agile wisdom, to joyful wisdom, to swift wisdom, to piercing wisdom, to penetrative wisdom. What is that one quality? It is mindfulness directed to the body. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one quality that, when cultivated and frequently practiced, leads to the acquisition of wisdom, to the growth of wisdom, to the expansion of wisdom, to great wisdom, to widespread wisdom, to abundant wisdom, to profound wisdom, to extraordinary wisdom, to vast wisdom, to ample wisdom, to quick wisdom, to agile wisdom, to joyful wisdom, to swift wisdom, to piercing wisdom, to penetrative wisdom.”
