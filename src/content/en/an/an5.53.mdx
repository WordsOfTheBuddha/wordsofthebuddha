---
title: Padhāniyaṅga sutta - Factors of Striving
description: "The Buddha outlines the five essential factors of striving: conviction in the Buddha’s awakening, physical health with balanced digestion, honesty without deceit, persistent energy, and penetrative wisdom that sees arising and passing away."
qualities: vigour, rousing of energy, sincerity, faith, wisdom
theme: wisdom
slug: an5.53
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five factors of striving. What five?

1.) Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu has faith; he has conviction in the awakening of the |Tathāgata::one who has arrived at the truth, an epithet of a perfectly Awakened One [tathāgata]|, acknowledging: ‘The Blessed One is an Arahant, a perfectly Awakened One, accomplished in true knowledge and conduct, who has reached the destination, knower of the world, an unsurpassed guide of trainable persons, a teacher of gods and humans, Buddha, Fortunate One.’

2.) He is without affliction and rarely ill, endowed with a balanced digestion that is neither too cold nor too hot, but moderate and suitable for striving.

3.) He is |neither deceitful::sincere [asaṭha]| |nor hypocritical::having a transparent character [amāyāvī]|, revealing himself as he truly is to the Teacher or to wise spiritual companions.

4.) He dwells with energy aroused for the abandoning of unwholesome qualities and the acquisition of wholesome qualities; he is |firm::powerful, having stamina [thāmavant]|, |steadfast in striving::having persistent effort [daḷhaparakkama]|, and |never lays down the yoke of::unwavering in his commitment to; lit. not put down the yoke pole [anikkhittadhura]| wholesome qualities.

5.) He is wise, endowed with |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]| that discerns the |arising and passing away::appearance and disappearance, formation and dissolution [udayatthagāmī]|, which is noble and penetrative, |leading to the complete cessation of suffering::leading to extinction of stress, leading to gradual and complete wearing away of discontentment [dukkhakkhayagāmī]|.

These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five factors of striving.”
