---
title: Bahubhāṇi sutta - One Who Speaks Much
description: Five drawbacks in speaking much and five benefits in speaking wisely.
qualities: wrong speech, right speech, wisdom, self-control
theme: principle, wisdom
slug: an5.214
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five |drawbacks::dangers [ādīnavā]| for a person who |speaks much::is talkative, talks excessively [bahubhāṇī]|. What five? They |speak falsely::lie, deliberately speak falsehood [musā bhaṇati]|, |speak divisively::speak slanderously to create division and sow discord [pisuṇaṁ bhaṇati]|, |speak harshly::speak using rough words, rudely, or in an unkind manner [pharusaṁ bhaṇati]|, |chatter frivolously::engage in idle chatter, meaningless talk, or gossip [samphappalāpaṁ bhaṇati]|, and upon the breaking up of the body, after death, they are reborn in a state of loss, in a bad destination, in a |realm of downfall::realm of misery [vinipāta]|, in |hell::a place of intense suffering, lit. no good fortune [niraya]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five drawbacks for a person who speaks much.

|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five |benefits::advantages, blessings, rewards [ānisaṁsā]| for a person who |speaks wisely::speaks thoughtfully, with deliberation [mantabhāṇī]|. What five? They do not speak falsely, do not speak divisively, do not speak harshly, do not chatter frivolously, and upon the breaking up of the body, after death, they are reborn in a good destination, a |heavenly world::a blissful realm, celestial world [sagga]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five benefits for a person who speaks wisely.”
