title: Santhāra vagga - The Chapter on Welcoming
description: The Buddha contrasts twelve pairs of material and Dhamma-based qualities and declares which is the best.
qualities: giving, quest, recollection of the Dhamma
theme: cultivating discernment, principle, wisdom

#### 2.151

“There are these two kinds of |welcoming::cordiality, fellowship, extending a welcome [santhāra]||, bhikkhus::::|. What two? Welcoming with |material things::worldly goods, physical requisites, things of the flesh [āmisa]| and welcoming with the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two kinds of welcoming. Of these two kinds of welcoming, |bhikkhus,::::| welcoming with the Dhamma is |the best::foremost, the highest [etadagga]|.”
