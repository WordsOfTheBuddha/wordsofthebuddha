#### 2.230–279

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two |unwholesome::unhealthy, unskillful, unbeneficial, or karmically unprofitable [akusala]| qualities ... there are these two |wholesome::healthy, skillful, beneficial, or karmically profitable [kusala]| qualities ... there are these two |blameworthy::faulty, reproachable [sāvajja]| qualities ... there are these two |blameless::faultless, irreproachable [anavajja]| qualities ... there are these two qualities |yielding suffering::having suffering as their outcome, leading to pain [dukkhudraya]| ... there are these two qualities |yielding happiness::having happiness as their outcome, leading to ease [sukhudraya]| ... there are these two qualities |resulting in suffering::ripening as pain, having painful kammic fruit [dukkhavipāka]| ... there are these two qualities |resulting in happiness::ripening as happiness, having pleasant kammic fruit [sukhavipāka]| ... there are these two |afflictive::troublesome, oppressive, bringing distress [sabyābajjha]| qualities ... there are these two |non-afflictive::free from distress, untroubled, peaceful [abyābajjha]| qualities. What two? Non-anger and non-resentment ... |non-contempt::freedom from disparagement or ingratitude [amakkha]| and |non-feuding::freedom from rivalry or contention [apaḷāsa]| ... non-jealousy and non-stinginess ... |non-hypocrisy::sincerity, transparency, openness [amāyā]| and |non-treachery::honesty, freedom from deviousness [asāṭheyya]| ... |sense of right and wrong::sense of shame, conscience, modesty [hirī]| and |moral dread::fear of wrongdoing out of regard for others [ottappa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two non-afflictive qualities.”
