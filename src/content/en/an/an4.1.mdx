Thus have I heard—At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling among the |Vajjians::name of the people of Vajji—a republic or confederation in ancient India, located in what is today northern Bihar, near the modern city of Vaishali [vajjī]| in the village of Bhaṇḍa. There the Blessed One addressed the bhikkhus: “Bhikkhus.”

“Venerable sir,” the bhikkhus replied. The Blessed One said this:

“|Bhikkhus,::::| it is because of |non-comprehension::misunderstanding [ananubodha]| and |non-penetration::lit. not piercing back [appaṭivedha]| of four |principles::characteristics [dhammā]| that both you and I have wandered on in |cyclic existence::wandering on from one state of existence to another, the cycle of birth and death, moving on continuously [saṁsāra]| for a long time. What four?

1.) |Bhikkhus,::::| it is because of non-comprehension and non-penetration of noble |ethical conduct::virtue, moral integrity [sīla]| that both you and I have wandered on in cyclic existence for a long time.

2.) |Bhikkhus,::::| it is because of non-comprehension and non-penetration of noble |collectedness::stability of mind, stillness of mind, mental composure [samādhi]| that both you and I have wandered on in cyclic existence for a long time.

3.) |Bhikkhus,::::| it is because of non-comprehension and non-penetration of noble |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]| that both you and I have wandered on in cyclic existence for a long time.

4.) |Bhikkhus,::::| it is because of non-comprehension and non-penetration of noble |liberation::release, deliverance, freedom, emancipation [vimutti]| that both you and I have wandered on in cyclic existence for a long time.

But now, |bhikkhus,::::| having comprehended and |penetrated::lit. pierced back [paṭividdha]| noble ethical conduct, noble collectedness, noble wisdom, and noble liberation; the |craving for existence::craving for continued existence, desire for a stable identity, attachment to a becoming, a future self, attainment, or experience [bhavataṇhā]| is severed, the conduit to existence is completely |exhausted::depleted, finished [khīṇa]|; there is now no more |renewed existence::renewal of being, reappearance, rebirth, future life [punabbhava]|.”

The Blessed One said this. Having spoken thus, the Accomplished One further said this:

“Ethical conduct, collectedness, wisdom,
and the |unsurpassed::highest, incomparable [anuttara]| liberation;
these principles have been comprehended
by Gotama, the illustrious one.

Thus, the Buddha, having |directly known::completely known, realized [abhiññāya]|,
clarified the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]| to the bhikkhus;
the teacher, the end maker of suffering,
the one with vision, is |completely quenched::completely cooled [parinibbuta]|.”
