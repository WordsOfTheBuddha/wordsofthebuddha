---
title: Catuttha hita sutta - Benefit (Fourth)
description: The Buddha describes a person who is personally accomplished in virtue, collectedness, wisdom, and liberation while actively encouraging others, as practicing for both their own benefit and others’.
qualities: ethical conduct, collectedness, wisdom, liberation, vision
theme: wisdom, inspiration
slug: an5.20
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu endowed with five qualities is practicing both for his own benefit and for the benefit of others. What five?

1.) Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu is personally |accomplished in virtue::of excellent morality; accomplished in ethical conduct [sīlasampanna]| and he |encourages::arouses, inspires, urges; lit. causes to take [samādapeti]| others in accomplishment in virtue;

2.) he is personally |accomplished in collectedness::accomplished in stability of mind, skilled in mental stillness [samādhisampanna]| and he encourages others in accomplishment in collectedness;

3.) he is personally accomplished in |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]| and he encourages others in accomplishment in wisdom;

4.) he is personally accomplished in |liberation::release, deliverance, freedom, emancipation [vimutti]| and he encourages others in accomplishment in liberation;

5.) he is personally accomplished in the |knowledge and vision of liberation::understanding and realization of liberation, total understanding of emancipation [vimuttiñāṇadassana]| and he encourages others in accomplishment in the knowledge and vision of liberation.

Endowed with these five qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu is practicing both for his own benefit and for the benefit of others.”
