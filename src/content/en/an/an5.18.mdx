---
title: Dutiya hita sutta - Benefit (Second)
description: The Buddha describes a person who urges others toward virtue, collectedness, wisdom, and liberation, without personally possessing these accomplishments, practices for the benefit of others, not themselves.
qualities: ethical conduct, collectedness, wisdom, liberation, vision
theme: wisdom, inspiration
slug: an5.18
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu endowed with five qualities is practicing for the benefit of others, not for his own benefit. What five?

1.) Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu is not personally |accomplished in virtue::of excellent morality; accomplished in ethical conduct [sīlasampanna]|, but |encourages::arouses, inspires, urges; lit. causes to take [samādapeti]| others in accomplishment in virtue;

2.) he is not personally |accomplished in collectedness::accomplished in stability of mind, skilled in mental stillness [samādhisampanna]|, but encourages others in accomplishment in collectedness;

3.) he is not personally accomplished in |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]|, but encourages others in accomplishment in wisdom;

4.) he is not personally accomplished in |liberation::release, deliverance, freedom, emancipation [vimutti]|, but encourages others in accomplishment in liberation;

5.) he is not personally accomplished in the |knowledge and vision of liberation::understanding and realization of liberation, total understanding of emancipation [vimuttiñāṇadassana]|, but encourages others in accomplishment in the knowledge and vision of liberation.

Endowed with these five qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu is practicing for the benefit of others, not for his own benefit.”
