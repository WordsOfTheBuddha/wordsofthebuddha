---
title: Sāvajja sutta - Blameworthy
description: The Buddha contrasts an immature person, recognized by blameworthy bodily, verbal, and mental action, with a wise person, recognized by blameless bodily, verbal, and mental action.
qualities: immaturity, wisdom, unwholesome, wholesome
theme: training guideline, cultivating discernment, principle
slug: an3.7
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| an |immature person::lacking in discernment or good sense, child-like in understanding [bāla]| should be recognized by three qualities. What are these three? |Blameworthy::faulty, objectionable, reproachable [sāvajja]| bodily action, blameworthy verbal action, and blameworthy mental action. <collapse>Indeed, bhikkhus, an immature person should be recognized by these three qualities. Bhikkhus, a wise person should be recognized by three qualities. What are these three?</collapse> |Blameless::faultless, irreproachable, unobjectionable [anavajja]| bodily action, blameless verbal action, and blameless mental action. <collapse>Indeed, bhikkhus, a wise person should be recognized by these three qualities. Therefore, bhikkhus, you should train thus: ‘Avoiding the three qualities by which an immature person is recognized, and adopting the three qualities by which a wise person is known, we will behave accordingly.’ Thus, bhikkhus, you should train.</collapse>”
