“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five |drawbacks::dangers [ādīnavā]| for one who falls asleep |muddle-minded::forgetful, not mindful [muṭṭhassatī]| and |lacking clear awareness::inattentive [asampajāna]|. What five? One |sleeps with discomfort::sleeps poorly, sleeps uneasily [dukkhaṁ supati]|, wakes up with discomfort, sees |worthless dreams::bad dreams, nightmares [pāpakaṁ supinaṁ]|, deities do not protect one, and |semen is emitted::impurity is discharged [asuci muccati]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five drawbacks for one who falls asleep muddle-minded and lacking clear awareness.

|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five |benefits::advantages, blessings, rewards [ānisaṁsā]| for one who falls asleep |with mindfulness established::mindful, with presence of mind [upaṭṭhitassatī]| and |clear awareness::with attentiveness, with clear and full comprehension, intentional, deliberate, purposeful [sampajāna]|. What five? One |sleeps with ease::sleeps comfortably, sleeps peacefully [sukhaṁ supati]|, wakes up with ease, does not see worthless dreams, deities protect one, and semen is not emitted. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five benefits for one who falls asleep with mindfulness established and clear awareness.”
