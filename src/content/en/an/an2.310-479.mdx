#### 2.310–319

“|Bhikkhus,::::| for the |direct knowledge::experiential understanding [abhiññāya]| of |passion::intense desire, strong emotion, infatuation, obsession, lust [rāga]|, two things should be cultivated. What two? |Tranquility::serenity, stilling, calming, mental unification [samatha]| and |penetrative vision::insight, seeing things distinctly as they are [vipassanā]|. |Bhikkhus,::::| for the direct knowledge of passion, these two things should be cultivated.”

“|Bhikkhus,::::| for the |complete comprehension::full understanding [pariññāya]| ... for the |complete exhaustion::gradual and complete wearing away [parikkhaya]| ... for the |giving up::letting go, abandoning [pahāna]| ... for the wearing away ... for the fading away ... for |dispassion::fading of desire [virāga]| ... for the cessation ... for the abandoning ... for the |relinquishment::letting go, complete release [paṭinissagga]| of passion, two things should be cultivated ...

#### 2.320–479

“Of |aversion::hatred, hostility, mental attitude of rejection, fault-finding, resentful disapproval [dosa]| ... of |delusion::illusion, misperception, erroneous belief, false idea, misapprehension; a fundamental distortion of reality that sustains confusion, clouds discernment, and fuels further doubt [moha]| ... of anger ... of resentment ... of |contempt::ungratefulness, depreciation, denigration, disrespect, belittlement, disparagement [makkha]| ... of |feuding::quarreling, rivalry, opposition, struggle, conflict [paḷāsa]| ... of jealousy ... of stinginess ... of deceitfulness ... of |treachery::conning, deviousness, scamming [sāṭheyya]| ... of stubbornness ... of |aggressiveness::hostile or violent behavior towards living beings [sārambha]| ... of |conceit::self-view expressed as comparison—seeing oneself as superior, inferior, or equal; the persistent “I am” conceit (asmimāna) that underlies identification [māna]| ... of arrogance ... of |vanity::indulgence, excess, pleasure, intoxication [mada]| ... of |negligence::carelessness, heedlessness [pamāda]|—for the direct knowledge ... for the full understanding ... for the complete exhaustion ... for the giving up ... for the wearing away ... for the fading away ... for dispassion ... for the cessation ... for the abandoning ... for the relinquishment [of negligence], two things should be cultivated. What two? Tranquility and penetrative vision. |Bhikkhus,::::| for the relinquishment of negligence, these two things should be cultivated.”

The Blessed One said this. The bhikkhus were delighted and pleased with the Blessed One’s words.
