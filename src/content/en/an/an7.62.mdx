“Do not fear |merit::good deeds [puññā]||, bhikkhus.::::.| Merit|, bhikkhus,::::| is an expression for |happiness::ease, contentment [sukha]|. I directly know|, bhikkhus,::::| that for a long time I have experienced the desired, lovely, and agreeable results of merit performed long ago. I cultivated a |mind of loving-kindness::mind of benevolence, friendly heart [mettacitta]| for seven years. Having cultivated a mind of loving-kindness for seven years, I did not return to this world for seven |aeons::an aeon is the lifespan of a world system, a vast cosmic time span [kappa]| of contraction and expansion. When the world was contracting, |bhikkhus,::::| I went to the realm of the |Gods of Streaming Radiance::MA: By mentioning these, all beings occupying the plane of the second jhāna — the Gods of Limited Radiance and the Gods of Immeasurable Radiance — should be included, for all these occupy a single level [ābhassara + devā]|. When the world was expanding, I was reborn in an empty Brahma mansion.

There|, bhikkhus,::::,| I was Brahmā, the Great Brahmā, the Conqueror, the Unconquered, the All-Seeing, the Wielder of Power. Thirty-six times|, bhikkhus,::::,| I was Sakka, lord of the gods. Many hundreds of times I was a |wheel-turning monarch::a just and virtuous ruler whose domain extends over the entire known world [rājā cakkavattī]|, a righteous king ruling by the Dhamma, a conqueror of the four quarters, having established stability in the realm, endowed with the seven treasures. And for me, |bhikkhus,::::| there were these seven treasures, namely: the wheel treasure, the elephant treasure, the horse treasure, the jewel treasure, the woman treasure, the householder treasure, and the counselor treasure as the seventh. Moreover, |bhikkhus,::::| I had more than a thousand sons who were valiant, heroic, able to crush the armies of their enemies. Having conquered this earth surrounded by the ocean without rod or sword, solely through the Dhamma, I ruled over it.

See the result of merit,
of skillful deeds, for one seeking happiness:
having cultivated a mind of loving-kindness
for seven years, bhikkhus,
for seven aeons of contraction and expansion,
I did not return to this world again.

When the world was contracting,
I went to the realm of Streaming Radiance;
when the world was expanding,
I reached an empty Brahma mansion.

Seven times I was the Great Brahmā,
and at that time the wielder of power;
thirty-six times the lord of the gods,
I exercised divine rule.

I was a wheel-turning monarch,
ruler of the Rose-Apple Land;
an anointed king of the noble warrior class,
I was sovereign over human beings.

Without rod and without sword,
having conquered this earth,
through non-violent deeds,
I governed it impartially.

Having ruled by the Dhamma
over this sphere of the earth,
I was born into a rich family,
with abundant wealth and possessions.

Endowed with all sensual pleasures,
and with the seven treasures;
the Buddhas, benefactors of the world—
by them this has been well taught:

This is the cause of greatness
whereby my earthly dominion does not fail;
rich in wealth and possessions,
a king radiant with majesty.

Possessed of psychic power and renown,
lord of the |Jambumaṇḍa::a reference to the Indian subcontinent, lit. land of the black plum aka jambu trees [jambumaṇḍa]|;
who, having heard this, would not be inspired,
even one of low birth?

Therefore, those desiring their own good,
aspiring for excellence,
should deeply revere the good Dhamma,
recollecting the Buddhas’ teaching.”
