---
title: Kammanidāna sutta - Causes of Kamma
description: From killing and false speech to ill will and wrong view, every unwholesome deed stems from greed, aversion, or delusion, and eliminating those three roots extinguishes the causes of kamma.
qualities: greed, aversion, delusion, ending
theme: wisdom, cultivating discernment
slug: an10.174
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |killing living beings::destroying life, taking life [pāṇātipāta]| to be threefold: rooted in |greed::a grasping mental quality of craving, possessiveness, or lustful wanting that clings to objects or experiences; it fuels attachment and obstructs renunciation and contentment [lobha]|, rooted in |aversion::hatred, hostility, mental attitude of rejection, fault-finding, resentful disapproval [dosa]|, and rooted in |delusion::illusion, misperception, erroneous belief, false idea, misapprehension; a fundamental distortion of reality that sustains confusion, clouds discernment, and fuels further doubt [moha]|.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |taking what is not given::stealing, theft [adinnādāna]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |sexual misconduct::wrong conduct in sensual pleasures [kāmesumicchācāra]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |false speech::lying, speaking untruth [musāvāda]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |divisive speech::malicious or slanderous speech that creates discord [pisuṇavācā]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |harsh speech::rough, abusive, or hurtful speech [pharusavācā]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |frivolous speech::idle chatter, meaningless talk [samphappalāpa]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |covetousness::longing for what belongs to others, envious craving [abhijjhā]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |ill will::malice, hostility, wishing harm upon others [byāpāda]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion.

|Bhikkhus,::::| I declare |wrong view::distorted or erroneous outlook on reality and kamma [micchādiṭṭhi]| to be threefold: rooted in greed, rooted in aversion, and rooted in delusion. Thus|, bhikkhus,::::| greed is the origin of the causes of |kamma::action, deed, doing, volitional act [kamma]|, aversion is the origin of the causes of kamma, and delusion is the origin of the causes of kamma. With the ending of greed comes the ending of the causes of kamma; with the ending of aversion comes the ending of the causes of kamma; with the ending of delusion comes the ending of the causes of kamma.”
