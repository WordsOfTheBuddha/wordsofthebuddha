---
title: Ñāta sutta - Well-Known
description: A well-known bhikkhu who encourages inappropriate bodily action, verbal action, and mental qualities practices for the detriment and unhappiness of many people, whereas one who encourages appropriate bodily action, verbal action, and mental qualities practices for the welfare and happiness of gods and humans.
qualities: harm, non-harm, suffering, happiness, wholesome, unwholesome, ethical conduct
theme: training guideline, principle
slug: an3.11
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with three qualities, a |well-known::prominent, recognized, famous [ñāta]| bhikkhu is practicing for the detriment of many people, for the unhappiness of many people, for the harm, detriment, and |suffering::discomfort, unpleasantness, discontentment, dissatisfaction, stress, pain, disease, i.e. mild or intense suffering [dukkha]| of many people, of gods and human beings. What are these three? He |encourages::urges, inspires, establishes others in [samādapeti]| |inappropriate::unsuitable, improper, not in line with the teaching [ananulomika]| bodily action, he encourages inappropriate verbal action, and he encourages inappropriate |mental qualities::states, principles, teachings [dhammā]|.

Endowed with these three qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| a well-known bhikkhu is practicing for the detriment of many people, for the unhappiness of many people, for the harm, detriment, and suffering of many people, of gods and human beings.

|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with three qualities, a well-known bhikkhu is practicing for the welfare of many people, for the happiness of many people, for the good, welfare, and happiness of many people, of gods and human beings. What are these three? He encourages |appropriate::suitable, proper, in line with the teaching [anulomika]| bodily action, he encourages appropriate verbal action, and he encourages appropriate mental qualities.

Endowed with these three qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| a well-known bhikkhu is practicing for the welfare of many people, for the happiness of many people, for the good, welfare, and happiness of many people, of gods and human beings.”
