#### 2.280

“|Bhikkhus,::::| on account of two reasons a training rule has been declared by the |Tathāgata::one who has arrived at the truth, an epithet of a perfectly Awakened One [tathāgata]| for his disciples.

What two? For the excellence of the |Saṅgha::The community of monks and nuns practicing in line with the Buddha’s teachings. In the broader sense, this is the community of disciples who have realized the noble path and fruition through the Buddha’s teachings [saṅgha]| and for the comfort of the Saṅgha ... for the restraint of |shameless::obstinate, difficult to admonish [dummaṅku]| persons and for the comfortable abiding of virtuous bhikkhus ... for the restraint of |taints::outflows, discharges; (comm) mental defilements or afflictions [āsavā]| pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of taints pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of hostilities pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of hostilities pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of faults pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of faults pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of fears pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of fears pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of |unwholesome::unhealthy, unskillful, unbeneficial, or karmically unprofitable [akusala]| qualities pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of unwholesome qualities pertaining to future lives ... out of compassion for householders and for cutting off the factions of bhikkhus with |evil desires::corrupt wishes, harmful yearnings [pāpiccha]| ... for inspiring |serene confidence::faith, trust, clarity of heart [pasāda]| in those without confidence and for the increase of those with confidence ... for the endurance of the |good Dhamma::good teaching, true nature of things [saddhamma]| and for the support of the |Vinaya::code of monastic discipline rules, training [vinaya]|. |Bhikkhus,::::| it is on account of these two reasons that a training rule has been declared by the Tathāgata for his disciples.”

#### 2.281–309

disciples ... the recitation of the Pātimokkha has been declared ... the suspension of the Pātimokkha has been declared ... the |Invitation::the Pavāraṇā ceremony at the end of the rains residence formal act of reconciliation has been declared ... the |formal act of suspension::disciplinary procedure of debarment from communion for not seeing an offense, not making amends, or not relinquishing a pernicious view [ukkhepanīyakamma]| has been declared ... the |granting of probation::disciplinary period of probation for a concealed saṅghādisesa offense [parivāsadāna]| has been declared ... with a motion and one announcement::Saṅgha procedure where the resolution is second, i.e., a motion followed by one proclamation [ñattidutiyakamma]| has been declared ... a |formal act with a motion and three announcements::Saṅgha procedure where the resolution is fourth, i.e., a motion followed by three proclamations [ñatticatutthakamma]| has been declared ... laying down what had not been What two? For the excellence of the Saṅgha and for the comfort of the Saṅgha ... for the restraint of shameless persons and for the comfortable abiding of virtuous bhikkhus ... for the restraint of taints pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of taints pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of hostilities pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of hostilities pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of faults pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of faults pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of fears pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of fears pertaining to future lives ... for the restraint of unwholesome qualities pertaining to this present life and for the warding off of unwholesome qualities pertaining to future lives ... out of compassion for householders and for cutting off the factions of bhikkhus with evil desires ... for inspiring serene confidence in those without confidence and for the increase of those with confidence ... for the endurance of the good Dhamma and for the support of the Vinaya.

|Bhikkhus,::::| it is on account of these two reasons that covering over with grass has been declared by the Tathāgata for his disciples.”
