title: Dukkhānupassī sutta - Observing Unsatisfactoriness

qualities: recognition of unsatisfactoriness

“Bhikkhus, these seven persons are worthy of offerings ... and are the unsurpassed field of merit for the world. What seven? Here, bhikkhus, a certain person dwells observing unsatisfactoriness in all conditions ...”
