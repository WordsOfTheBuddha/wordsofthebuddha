#### 1.21

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when uncultivated is as |ineffective::not ready or fit for any act or use [akammaniya]| as the mind. An uncultivated mind|, bhikkhus,::::| is ineffective.”

#### 1.22

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when cultivated is as |effective::ready, workable, suitable for use [kammaniya]| as the mind. A cultivated mind|, bhikkhus,::::| is effective.”

#### 1.23

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when uncultivated leads to as much |harm::misfortune, damage, injury, hurt, detriment [anattha]| as the mind. An uncultivated mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great harm.”

#### 1.24

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when cultivated leads to as much |benefit::good, welfare, profit [attha]| as the mind. A cultivated mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great benefit.”

#### 1.25

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when uncultivated and |uncleared::not evident [apātubhūta]|, leads to as much harm as the mind. An uncultivated and uncleared mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great harm.”

#### 1.26

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when cultivated and |cleared::becomes evident [pātubhūta]|, leads to as much benefit as the mind. A cultivated and cleared mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great benefit.”

#### 1.27

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when uncultivated and infrequently practiced leads to as much harm as the mind. An uncultivated and infrequently practiced mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great harm.”

#### 1.28

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when cultivated and frequently practiced leads to as much benefit as the mind. A cultivated and frequently practiced mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great benefit.”

#### 1.29

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when uncultivated and infrequently practiced leads to as much suffering as the mind. An uncultivated and infrequently practiced mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great suffering.”

#### 1.30

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I do not see any other single thing that when cultivated and frequently practiced leads to as much |contentment::ease, happiness, pleasant abiding [sukha]| as the mind. A cultivated and frequently practiced mind|, bhikkhus,::::| leads to great ease.”
