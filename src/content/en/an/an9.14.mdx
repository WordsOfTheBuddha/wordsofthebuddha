Then the venerable Samiddhi approached the venerable |Sāriputta::foremost disciple of the Buddha in great wisdom [sāriputta]|; having approached, he paid homage to the venerable Sāriputta and sat down to one side. When the venerable Samiddhi was seated to one side, the venerable Sāriputta said to him:

“With what as their |object::basis, support [ārammaṇa]|, Samiddhi, do |intentions and thoughts::purposes and reflections, directed thoughts [saṅkappavitakkā]| arise in a person?”

“With |name and form::mentality and materiality [nāmarūpa]| as their object, venerable sir.”

“And where, Samiddhi, do they become |diverse::varied, manifold [nānatta]|?”

“In the |elements::the eighteen sensory elements or underlying properties of experience [dhātū]|, venerable sir.”

“And from what, Samiddhi, do they arise?”

“They arise from |contact::sense impingement, raw experience, touch [phassa]|, venerable sir.”

“And upon what, Samiddhi, do they converge?”

“They converge upon |feeling::pleasant, neutral, or painful sensation, the experience felt on contact through eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind; second of the five aggregates [vedanā]|, venerable sir.”

“And by what, Samiddhi, are they headed?”

“They are headed by |collectedness::stability of mind, stillness of mind, mental composure [samādhi]|, venerable sir.”

“And what, Samiddhi, exercises authority over them?”

“|Mindfulness::active presence of mind and lucid retention, recollection [sati]| exercises authority over them, venerable sir.”

“And by what, Samiddhi, are they surpassed?”

“They are surpassed by |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]|, venerable sir.”

“And what, Samiddhi, is their |essence::core, heartwood [sāra]|?”

“|Liberation::release, deliverance, freedom [vimutti]| is their essence, venerable sir.”

“And what, Samiddhi, are they grounded upon?”

“They are grounded upon the |deathless::deathless state, epithet of Nibbāna [amata]|, venerable sir.”

“Being asked: ‘With what as their object, Samiddhi, do intentions and thoughts arise in a person?’ you say: ‘With name and form as their object, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And where, Samiddhi, do they become diverse?’ you say: ‘In the elements, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And from what, Samiddhi, do they arise?’ you say: ‘They arise from contact, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And upon what, Samiddhi, do they converge?’ you say: ‘They converge upon feeling, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And by what, Samiddhi, are they headed?’ you say: ‘They are headed by collectedness, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And what, Samiddhi, exercises authority over them?’ you say: ‘Mindfulness exercises authority over them, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And by what, Samiddhi, are they surpassed?’ you say: ‘They are surpassed by wisdom, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And what, Samiddhi, is their essence?’ you say: ‘Liberation is their essence, venerable sir.’ Being asked: ‘And what, Samiddhi, are they grounded upon?’ you say: ‘They are grounded upon the deathless, venerable sir.’ Good, good, Samiddhi! It is good, Samiddhi, how you have answered each question put to you—yet |do not be conceited on account of that::do not think highly of yourself or form self-conceit because of that [tena ca mā maññi]|.”
