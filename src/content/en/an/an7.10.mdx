---
title: Macchariya sutta - Stinginess
description: The Buddha lists the seven fetters that bind beings.
qualities: aversion, doubt, conceit, jealousy, stinginess
theme: principle
slug: an7.10
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these seven fetters. What seven?

The |fetter of attraction::bond of affection [anunayasaṁyojana]|, the |fetter of repulsion::fetter of resistance [paṭighasaṁyojana]|, the |fetter of view::bond of belief [diṭṭhisaṁyojana]|, the fetter of doubt, the |fetter of conceit::chain of superiority [mānasaṁyojana]|, the |fetter of jealousy::bond of envy [issāsaṁyojana]|, the |fetter of stinginess::bond of tight-fistedness [macchariyasaṁyojana]|.

These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the seven fetters.”
