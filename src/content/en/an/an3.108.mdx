---
title: Atitti sutta - No Satisfaction
description: Indulging in sleep, consuming intoxicants, and sexual intercourse gives no satisfaction.
qualities: discontentment, drowsiness, consuming intoxicants, sexual misconduct, sleep
theme: wisdom, principle, cultivating discernment
slug: an3.108
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| indulging in these three things gives no |satisfaction::contentment, satiation [titti]|. What three? 1.) Indulging in sleep gives no satisfaction. 2.) |Consuming intoxicants::drinking alcohol, drinking liquor and wine, taking intoxicating substances [surāmerayapāna]| gives no satisfaction. 3.) Indulging in |sexual intercourse::engaging in sexual activity [methunadhammasamāpatti]| gives no satisfaction. Indulging in these three things|, bhikkhus,::::| gives no satisfaction.”
