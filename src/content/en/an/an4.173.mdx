Then the venerable |Mahākoṭṭhika::foremost disciple of the Buddha in analytical understanding [Mahākoṭṭhika]| approached the venerable |Sāriputta::foremost disciple of the Buddha in great wisdom [sāriputta]|. Having approached, he exchanged friendly greetings with the venerable Sāriputta. After the exchange of a courteous and polite conversation, he sat down to one side. Seated to one side, the venerable Mahākoṭṭhika said this to the venerable Sāriputta:

“Friend, with the |remainderless fading away and cessation::complete fading away and ending [asesavirāganirodha]| of the |six fields of contact::six fields of experience, six sense bases [phassāyatana]|, is there anything else?”

“Do not say so, friend.”

“Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there nothing else?”

“Do not say so, friend.”

“Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there both something else and nothing else?”

“Do not say so, friend.”

“Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there neither something else nor nothing else?”

“Do not say so, friend.”

“‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there anything else?’ When asked thus, you say: ‘Do not say so, friend.’ ‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there nothing else?’ When asked thus, you say: ‘Do not say so, friend.’ ‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there both something else and nothing else?’ When asked thus, you say: ‘Do not say so, friend.’ ‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there neither something else nor nothing else?’ When asked thus, you say: ‘Do not say so, friend.’ How then, friend, should the meaning of this statement be seen?”

“‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there anything else?’ In saying this, one |mentally proliferates::forms various opinions about [papañceti]| that |which cannot be conceptualized::which cannot be conceptualized [appapañca]|. ‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there nothing else?’ In saying this, one mentally proliferates that which cannot be conceptualized. ‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there both something else and nothing else?’ In saying this, one mentally proliferates that which cannot be conceptualized. ‘Friend, with the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, is there neither something else nor nothing else?’ In saying this, one mentally proliferates that which cannot be conceptualized. Friend, as far as the range of the six fields of contact extends, so far extends the range of |mental proliferation::various opinions, endless conceptualization [papañca]|; as far as the range of mental proliferation extends, so far extends the range of the six fields of contact. With the remainderless fading away and cessation of the six fields of contact, there is the cessation of mental proliferation, the |stilling::calming, subsiding [vūpasama]| of mental proliferation.”
