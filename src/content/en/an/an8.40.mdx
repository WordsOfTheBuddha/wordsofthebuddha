---
title: Duccaritavipāka sutta - Result of Misconduct
description: The Buddha explains the karmic results of breaking the five precepts and engaging in wrong speech. Even the lightest result brings specific corresponding suffering in a human birth, such as a short lifespan.
qualities: slaughtering, stealing, sexual misconduct, wrong speech, consuming intoxicants, negligence
theme: wisdom, cultivating discernment
priority: 1.2
slug: an8.40
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| the killing of living beings, when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of the killing of living beings is that it leads to a short lifespan when reborn as a human.

Taking what is not given, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of taking what is not given is that it leads to a loss of wealth when reborn as a human.

Sexual misconduct, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of sexual misconduct is that it leads to enmity and rivalry when reborn as a human.

|False speech::lying, deliberately speaking falsehood [musāvāda]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of false speech is that it leads to being falsely accused when reborn as a human.

|Divisive speech::slanderous, defamatory, malicious speech [pisuṇavācā]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of divisive speech is that it leads to being divided from friends when reborn as a human.

|Harsh speech::abusive, rude, or unkind way of speaking [pharusāvācā]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of harsh speech is that it leads to hearing disagreeable sounds when reborn as a human.

|Frivolous chatter::meaningless talk, gossip, idle speech [samphappalāpa]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of frivolous chatter is that it leads to one’s words not being taken seriously when reborn as a human.

Drinking spirits and alcoholic beverages, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of drinking spirits and alcoholic beverages is that it leads to madness when reborn as a human.”
