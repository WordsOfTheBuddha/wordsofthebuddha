---
title: Duccaritavipāka sutta - Result of Misconduct
description: The Buddha explains the karmic results of breaking the five precepts and engaging in wrong speech. Even the lightest result for a human being brings specific corresponding suffering, such as a short lifespan.
qualities: slaughtering, stealing, sexual misconduct, wrong speech, consuming intoxicants, negligence
theme: wisdom, cultivating discernment
priority: 1.2
slug: an8.40
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| the |killing of living beings::destruction of life [pāṇātipāta]|, when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of the killing of living beings for a human being is that it leads to a short lifespan.

|Taking what is not given::theft, stealing, taking what is not offered [adinnādāna]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of taking what is not given for a human being is that it leads to a loss of wealth.

|Sexual misconduct::wrong behaviour in sexual matters [kāmesumicchācāra]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of sexual misconduct for a human being is that it leads to enmity and rivalry.

|False speech::lying, deliberately speaking falsehood [musāvāda]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of false speech for a human being is that it leads to being falsely accused.

|Divisive speech::slanderous, defamatory, malicious speech [pisuṇavācā]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of divisive speech for a human being is that it leads to being divided from friends.

|Harsh speech::abusive, rude, or unkind way of speaking [pharusāvācā]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of harsh speech for a human being is that it leads to [hearing] disagreeable sounds.

|Frivolous chatter::meaningless talk, gossip, idle speech [samphappalāpa]|, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of frivolous chatter for a human being is that it leads to one’s words not being taken seriously.

Drinking spirits and alcoholic beverages, |bhikkhus,::::| when pursued, cultivated, and frequently practiced, leads to hell, leads to the animal realm, leads to the ghost realm. The lightest result of drinking spirits and alcoholic beverages for a human being is that it leads to madness.”
