---
title: Mahāli sutta - With Mahāli
description: The Buddha explains to Mahāli that bad kamma is rooted in greed, hatred, delusion, superficial attention, and a wrongly directed mind. Good kamma stems from their opposites, including radical attention, which together distinguish unrighteous conduct from righteous conduct.
qualities: greed, aversion, delusion, superficial attention, unwholesome, contentment, loving-kindness, wisdom, radical attention, wholesome
theme: wisdom, cultivating discernment
slug: an10.47
priority: 1.1
character: Mahāli the Licchavi
---

At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling at |Vesāli::capital of the Licchavīs [vesālī]|, in the |Great Forest::name of a forest outside Vesāli; lit. great forest [mahāvana]|, in the hall with the peaked roof. Then Mahāli the Licchavi approached the Blessed One. Having approached, he paid homage to the Blessed One and sat down to one side. Seated to one side, Mahāli the Licchavi said to the Blessed One:

“What is the cause, venerable sir, what is the condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma?”

“|Greed::a grasping mental quality of craving, possessiveness, or lustful wanting that clings to objects or experiences; it fuels attachment and obstructs renunciation and contentment [lobha]|, Mahāli, is a cause, greed is a condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma. |Hatred::aversion, hatred, hostility, mental attitude of rejection, fault-finding, resentful disapproval [dosa]|, Mahāli, is a cause, hatred is a condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma. |Delusion::illusion, misperception, erroneous belief, false idea, misapprehension; a fundamental distortion of reality that sustains confusion, clouds discernment, and fuels further doubt [moha]|, Mahāli, is a cause, delusion is a condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma. |Superficial attention::unwise attention, peripheral attention, content-bound reflection; lit. attention not from the source [ayonisomanasikāra]|, Mahāli, is a cause, superficial attention is a condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma. A |wrongly directed::badly oriented [micchāpaṇihita]| mind, Mahāli, is a cause, a wrongly directed mind is a condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma. This, Mahāli, is the cause, this is the condition for the doing of bad kamma, for the performance of bad kamma.

“But what, venerable sir, is the cause, what is the condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma?”

“|Non-greed::the absence of greed, lack of craving or lustful wanting; contentment, renunciation [alobha]|, Mahāli, is a cause, non-greed is a condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma. Non-hatred, Mahāli, is a cause, non-hatred is a condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma. |Non-delusion::the absence of illusion, misapprehension, or distorted view; clear comprehension, understanding things as they really are [amoha]|, Mahāli, is a cause, non-delusion is a condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma. |Radical attention::wise steering of attention, root-level attention, attention to the structural source, contextual reflection; lit. attention from the source [yoniso + manasikāra]|, Mahāli, is a cause, radical attention is a condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma. A |rightly directed::correctly oriented [sammāpaṇihita]| mind, Mahāli, is a cause, a rightly directed mind is a condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma. This, Mahāli, is the cause, this is the condition for the doing of good kamma, for the performance of good kamma.

If, Mahāli, these ten things did not exist in the world, unrighteous and discordant conduct would not be discerned here, nor would righteous and harmonious conduct be discerned. But because, Mahāli, these ten things exist in the world, therefore unrighteous and discordant conduct is discerned, and righteous and harmonious conduct is discerned.”
