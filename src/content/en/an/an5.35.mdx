“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five |benefits::advantages, blessings, rewards [ānisaṁsa]| of |giving::generosity, donating, charity [dāna]|. What five?

1.) One is dear and pleasing to many people; 2.) Good persons associate with one; 3.) a good reputation spreads about one; 4.) one does not stray from a householder’s duties; and 5.) when the body breaks up, after death, one is reborn in a good destination, a |heavenly world::a blissful realm, celestial world [sagga]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five benefits of giving.

One who gives is dear,
following the way of the good;
the good |restrained::self-controlled, disciplined [saññata]| |spiritual practitioners::those living the pure spiritual life [brahmacārī]|
always associate with one.

They teach one the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]|
that |dispels all suffering::drives out all dissatisfaction [sabbadukkhāpanūdana]|;
having |understood::directly known, realized [aññāya]| that Dhamma,
the taintless one here |dies their final death::attains final Nibbāna, is completely quenched [parinibbāti]|.”
