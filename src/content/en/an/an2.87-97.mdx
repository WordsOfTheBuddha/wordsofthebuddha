#### 2.87

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Liberation of mind::mental liberation, emancipation of heart, samādhi obtained from fruition [cetovimutti]| and |liberation by wisdom::emancipation by insight [paññāvimutti]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.88

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Exertion::energetic application, striving, active effort [paggāha]| and |non-distraction::unscatteredness, focus, absence of mental wandering [avikkhepa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.89

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Name::mentality, mental phenomena [nāma]| and |form::materiality, physical phenomena [rūpa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.90

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |True knowledge::wisdom, the direct realization of truth [vijjā]| and |liberation::release, deliverance, freedom, emancipation [vimutti]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.91

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? The |view of existence::the view of becoming, eternalism; belief in eternal continuation of self [bhavadiṭṭhi]| and the |view of non-existence::annihilationism; belief in total destruction at death [vibhavadiṭṭhi]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.92

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Lacking sense of right and wrong::shamelessness, lacking conscience [ahirika]| and |moral recklessness::fearlessness of wrongdoing, lack of prudence [anottappa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.93

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Sense of right and wrong::sense of shame, conscience, modesty [hirī]| and |moral dread::fear of wrongdoing out of regard for others [ottappa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.94

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Unruliness::not being amenable to verbal correction; lit. hard to talk to state [dovacassatā]| and |bad friendship::friendship with unwholesome persons [pāpamittatā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.95

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Being easy to instruct::amenability to correction [sovacassatā]| and |good friendship::friendship with wholesome persons [kalyāṇamittatā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.96

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Skill in elements::expertise in the eighteen sensory elements or other kinds of elements. See [MN 115](/mn115#skilled-in-the-elements) for a detailed treatment. [dhātukusalatā]| and |skill in attention::expertise in comprehending attention, its arising, ceasing and way of practice to the ceasing of attention [manasikārakusalatā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.97

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Skill in discerning what is an offense::expertise regarding monastic transgressions and rules [āpattikusalatā]| and |skill in rehabilitation from offenses::expertise in clearing or emerging from disciplinary transgressions [āpattivuṭṭhānakusalatā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”
