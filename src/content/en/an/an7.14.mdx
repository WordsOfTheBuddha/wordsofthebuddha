---
title: Puggala sutta - Seven Persons
description: The Buddha identifies seven types of persons worthy of offerings who act as the world's unsurpassed field of merit, including those liberated by wisdom and Dhamma-followers.
qualities: stream entry, faith, liberation
theme: principle
slug: an7.14
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| these seven persons are worthy of offerings, worthy of hospitality, worthy of gifts, worthy of reverential salutation, and are the unsurpassed field of merit for the world. What seven? One |liberated in both ways::emancipated in two regards [ubhatobhāgavimutta]|, one |liberated by wisdom::emancipated by insight [paññāvimutta]|, a |body-witness::liberated through the experience of jhāna and formless attainments [kāyasakkhī]|, one attained to view, one |attained through faith::liberated through faith; epithet of a stream-enterer or higher [saddhāvimutta]|, a |Dhamma-follower::who has attained the path of stream entry through understanding the teaching; lit. following the Dhamma [dhammānusārī]|, and a |faith-follower::who has attained the path of stream entry through faith; lit. faith follower [saddhānusārī]|. These seven persons, bhikkhus, are worthy of offerings, worthy of hospitality, worthy of gifts, worthy of reverential salutation, and are the unsurpassed field of merit for the world.”
