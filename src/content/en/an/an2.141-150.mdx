---
title: Dāna vagga - The Chapter on Giving
description: The Buddha contrasts ten pairs of material and Dhamma-based generosity—giving, offerings, generosity, charity, wealth, shared enjoyment, sharing, support, assistance, and sympathy—declaring the Dhamma-based kind the best.
qualities: giving, generosity, compassion, recollection of the Dhamma
theme: cultivating discernment, principle, inspiration
slug: an2.141-150
---

#### 2.141

“There are these two kinds of |giving::the act of giving or donating, with an intention to give [dāna]||, bhikkhus::::|. What two? The giving of |material things::worldly goods, physical requisites, things of the flesh [āmisa]| and the giving of the |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two kinds of giving. Of these two kinds of giving, |bhikkhus,::::| the giving of the Dhamma is |the best::foremost, the highest [etadagga]|.”
