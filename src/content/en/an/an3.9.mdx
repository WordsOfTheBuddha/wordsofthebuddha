---
title: Khata sutta - Maimed
description: Endowed with bodily, verbal, and mental misconduct, an immature person maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state and accumulates much demerit; endowed with good bodily, verbal, and mental conduct, a wise person of integrity maintains themselves unmaimed and accumulates much merit.
qualities: immaturity, wisdom, person of integrity, ethical conduct, unprincipled conduct, merit
theme: principle, cultivating discernment
slug: an3.9
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with three qualities, an |immature::lacking in discernment or good sense, child-like in understanding [bāla]|, |incompetent::unaccomplished, undistinguished, unskilled [abyatta]| |person lacking in integrity::unethical person, inferior person [asappurisa]| maintains themselves in a |maimed::injured, wounded, dug up [khata]| and |damaged::ruined, spoiled, impaired [upahata]| state, is |blameworthy::faulty, objectionable [sāvajja]| and |subject to reproach::liable to censure, criticizable [sānuvajja]| by the |wise::discerning, intelligent, who can distinguish [viññū]|, and accumulates much |demerit::unwholesome kamma, bad fortune [apuñña]|. What are these three? Bodily misconduct, verbal misconduct, and mental misconduct.

Endowed with these three qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| an immature, incompetent person lacking in integrity maintains themselves in a maimed and damaged state, is blameworthy and subject to reproach by the wise, and accumulates much demerit.

|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with three qualities, a |wise::astute, intelligent, learned, skilled [paṇḍita]|, |competent::accomplished, capable, learned [viyatta]| |person of integrity::virtuous person, true person [sappurisa]| maintains themselves in an |unmaimed::uninjured, intact [akkhata]| and |undamaged::unspoiled, unimpaired [anupahata]| state, is |blameless::faultless, unobjectionable [anavajja]| and |beyond reproach::above censure, irreproachable [ananuvajja]| by the wise, and accumulates much |merit::wholesome kamma, good fortune [puñña]|. What are these three? Good bodily conduct, good verbal conduct, and good mental conduct.

Endowed with these three qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| a wise, competent person of integrity maintains themselves in an unmaimed and undamaged state, is blameless and beyond reproach by the wise, and accumulates much merit.”
