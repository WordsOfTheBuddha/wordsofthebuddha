At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling at Sāvatthi, in the Eastern Park, at Migāramātā’s mansion. Then |Migāramātā Visākhā::one of the foremost laywomen and devoted supporters of the Buddha, also known as Migāramātā (Migāra’s mother) from a significant event in her life when she skillfully guided her father-in-law, Migāra, towards the Dhamma [visākha + migāramātar]| approached the Blessed One ... When she was seated to one side, the Blessed One said to Migāramātā Visākhā:

#### Success in This Life

“Visākhā, a woman endowed with four qualities practices for victory in this present life and her life in this world is successful. What four? Here, Visākhā, a woman is well-organized in her work, manages the domestic staff, conducts herself pleasingly toward her husband, and looks after his earnings.

1.) And how, Visākhā, is a woman well-organized in her work? Here, Visākhā, a woman is skillful and |industrious::energetic, active, not lazy [analasa]| in attending to her husband’s domestic chores, whether knitting or weaving; she is endowed with sound judgment about them in order to carry out and organize them properly. That is how, Visākhā, a woman is well-organized in her work.

2.) And how, Visākhā, does a woman manage the domestic staff? Here, Visākhā, regarding her husband’s domestic staff, whether bondservants, messengers, or workers, a woman knows what has been done as done and what has not been done as not done; she knows the condition of those who are ill; and she distributes to each an appropriate portion of edible and cooked food. That is how, Visākhā, a woman manages the domestic staff.

3.) And how, Visākhā, does a woman conduct herself pleasingly toward her husband? Here, Visākhā, a woman does not commit any action that her husband would consider disagreeable, even for the sake of her own life. That is how, Visākhā, a woman conducts herself pleasingly toward her husband.

4.) And how, Visākhā, does a woman look after her husband’s earnings? Here, Visākhā, whatever wealth, grain, |silver::cash, coin [rajata]|, or gold her husband brings home, she manages to keep secure through watchfulness and protection, and in regard to it she is not a spendthrift, not a thief, not a drunkard, and not a squanderer. That is how, Visākhā, a woman looks after her husband’s earnings.

A woman endowed with these four qualities practices for victory in this present life and her life in this world is successful.

#### Success in the Next Life

A woman endowed with four qualities practices for victory in the next life and her life in the next world is successful. What four? Here, Visākhā, a woman is accomplished in faith, accomplished in ethical conduct, accomplished in generosity, and accomplished in wisdom.

5.) And how, Visākhā, is a woman accomplished in faith? Here, Visākhā, a woman has faith; she places faith in the awakening of the |Tathāgata::one who has arrived at the truth, an epithet of a perfectly Awakened One [tathāgata]|, acknowledging: ‘The Blessed One is an |Arahant::a worthy one, a fully awakened being, epithet of the Buddha [arahant]|, a perfectly Awakened One, accomplished in true knowledge and conduct, who has reached the destination, knower of the world, an unsurpassed guide of trainable persons, a teacher of gods and humans, Buddha, Fortunate One.’ That is how, Visākhā, a woman is accomplished in faith.

6.) And how, Visākhā, is a woman accomplished in ethical conduct? Here, Visākhā, a woman abstains from |killing living beings::taking life, destruction of life [pāṇātipāta]|<collapse>, abstains from taking what is not given, abstains from sexual misconduct, abstains from false speech,</collapse> and abstains from |consuming intoxicants::consuming wine, spirits, or drugs and getting intoxicated [surāmerayamajja]| that are the basis for |negligence::heedlessness, carelessness [pamāda]|. That is how, Visākhā, a woman is accomplished in ethical conduct.

7.) And how, Visākhā, is a woman accomplished in generosity? Here, Visākhā, a woman dwells at home with a mind free from the stain of stinginess, freely generous, open-handed, delighting in relinquishment, responsive to requests, delighting in sharing gifts. That is how, Visākhā, a woman is accomplished in generosity.

8.) And how, Visākhā, is a woman accomplished in wisdom? Here, Visākhā, a woman is wise<collapse>, endowed with wisdom regarding arising and passing away, which is noble, penetrative, and leads to the complete destruction of suffering</collapse>. That is how, Visākhā, a woman is accomplished in wisdom.

A woman endowed with these four qualities practices for victory in the next life and her life in the next world is successful.

#### Verse

Well-organized in her work,
managing the domestic staff;
she treats her husband in agreeable ways
and looks after the earnings.

Faithful, endowed with virtue,
generous, free from stinginess;
she constantly purifies the path
that leads to safety in the future life.

For any woman
in whom these eight qualities are found,
they call her virtuous, firm in the Dhamma,
a speaker of truth.

Accomplished in sixteen aspects,^[1]^
well-endowed with the eight factors;
such a virtuous female lay disciple
is reborn in an agreeable heavenly world.”
