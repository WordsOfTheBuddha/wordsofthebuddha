---
title: Cintī sutta - Thinking
description: The Buddha explains the three characteristics, signs, and patterns of conduct by which the wise recognize an immature person or a wise person—how they think, speak, and act.
qualities: immaturity, wisdom, person of integrity, wrong speech, right speech, wholesome, unwholesome
theme: training guideline, cultivating discernment
slug: an3.3
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these three |characteristics::defining marks, traits [lakkhaṇa]|, |signs::indications, markers [nimitta]|, and patterns of conduct of an |immature person::lacking in discernment or good sense, child-like in understanding [bāla]|. What three? Here, |bhikkhus,::::| an immature person |thinks bad thoughts::thinks what is poorly thought [duccintitacintī]|, speaks poorly, and acts poorly. If|, bhikkhus,::::| an immature person did not think bad thoughts, speak poorly, and act poorly, how would the |wise::astute, intelligent, learned, skilled [paṇḍita]| know of them: ‘This fellow is an immature person, a |person lacking in integrity::unethical person, untrue person, inferior person [asappurisa]|’? But because|, bhikkhus,::::| an immature person thinks bad thoughts, speaks poorly, and acts poorly, the wise know of them: ‘This fellow is an immature person, a person lacking in integrity.’ These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the three characteristics, signs, and patterns of conduct of an immature person.

|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these three characteristics, signs, and patterns of conduct of a wise person. What three? Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a wise person |thinks good thoughts::thinks what is well thought [sucintitacintī]|, speaks well, and acts well. If|, bhikkhus,::::| a wise person did not think good thoughts, speak well, and act well, how would the wise know of them: ‘This worthy one is a wise person, a |person of integrity::virtuous person, true person [sappurisa]|’? But because|, bhikkhus,::::| a wise person thinks good thoughts, speaks well, and acts well, the wise know of them: ‘This worthy one is a wise person, a person of integrity.’ These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the three characteristics, signs, and patterns of conduct of a wise person. Therefore, |bhikkhus,::::| <collapse>you should train thus: ‘Avoiding the three qualities by which an immature person is recognized, and adopting the three qualities by which a wise person is known, we will behave accordingly.’ This is how|, bhikkhus,::::| you should train.</collapse>”
