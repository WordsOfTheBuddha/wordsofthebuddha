---
title: Paṭhama hita sutta - Benefit (First)
description: The Buddha describes a person who is accomplished in virtue, collectedness, wisdom, and liberation, yet failing to encourage others, practices solely for their own benefit.
qualities: ethical conduct, collectedness, wisdom, liberation, vision
theme: wisdom, inspiration
slug: an5.17
priority: 1.1
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu endowed with five qualities is practicing for his own benefit, not for the benefit of others. What five?

1.) Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu is personally |accomplished in virtue::of excellent morality; accomplished in ethical conduct [sīlasampanna]| but does not |encourage::arouses, inspires, urges; lit. causes to take [samādapeti]| others in accomplishment in virtue;

2.) he is personally |accomplished in collectedness::accomplished in stability of mind, skilled in mental stillness [samādhisampanna]| but does not encourage others in accomplishment in collectedness;

3.) he is personally accomplished in |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [paññā]| but does not encourage others in accomplishment in wisdom;

4.) he is personally accomplished in |liberation::release, deliverance, freedom, emancipation [vimutti]| but does not encourage others in accomplishment in liberation;

5.) he is personally accomplished in the |knowledge and vision of liberation::understanding and realization of liberation, total understanding of emancipation [vimuttiñāṇadassana]| but does not encourage others in accomplishment in the knowledge and vision of liberation.

Endowed with these five qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu is practicing for his own benefit, not for the benefit of others.”
