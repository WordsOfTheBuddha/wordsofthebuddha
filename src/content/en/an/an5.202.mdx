“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five benefits in listening to the Dhamma. What five?

One hears what has not been heard, clarifies what has been heard, overcomes doubt, straightens one’s view, and one’s mind becomes serene.

These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five benefits in listening to the Dhamma.”
