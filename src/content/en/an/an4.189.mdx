“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these four things |to be realized::to be personally experienced; lit. to be personally done [sacchikaraṇīya]|. What four?

There are|, bhikkhus,::::| things to be realized |with the body::personally, through direct meditative experience [kāyena]|; there are|, bhikkhus,::::| things to be realized through |mindfulness::active presence of mind and lucid retention, recollection, keeping in mind [sati]|; there are|, bhikkhus,::::| things to be realized through the |eye::faculty of seeing, vision [cakkhu]|; there are|, bhikkhus,::::| things to be realized through |wisdom::distinctive knowledge, discernment [pañña]|.

And what|, bhikkhus,::::| are the things to be realized with the body? The |eight liberations::three form-based releases (seeing forms while possessed of form; seeing external forms without perceiving form internally; resolved only upon the beautiful), then the four formless bases, then the cessation of perception and what is felt [aṭṭha vimokkhā]||, bhikkhus,::::| are to be realized with the body.

And what|, bhikkhus,::::| are the things to be realized through mindfulness? |Past lives::former abodes, previous existences [pubbenivāsa]||, bhikkhus,::::| are to be realized through mindfulness.

And what|, bhikkhus,::::| are the things to be realized through the eye? The |passing away and reappearance::the death and rebirth of beings according to their kamma [cutūpapāta]| of beings|, bhikkhus,::::| is to be realized through the eye.

And what|, bhikkhus,::::| are the things to be realized through wisdom? The |wearing away of the taints::gradual exhaustion and elimination of the mental defilements or deep-seated afflictions such as sensual desire, craving for existence, views, and ignorance [āsava + khaya]||, bhikkhus,::::| is to be realized through wisdom.

These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the four things to be realized.”
