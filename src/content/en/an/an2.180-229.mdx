#### 2.180–184

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two qualities. What two? |Anger::rage, wrath, fury, indignation [kodha]| and |resentment::bearing a grudge, harboring enmity [upanāha]| ... |contempt::ungratefulness, depreciation, denigration, disrespect, belittlement, disparagement [makkha]| and |feuding::quarreling, rivalry, opposition, struggle, conflict [paḷāsa]| ... jealousy and stinginess ... |hypocrisy::trickery, deceitfulness, dishonesty [māyā]| and |treachery::conning, deviousness, scamming [sāṭheyya]| ... |lacking sense of right and wrong::shamelessness, lacking conscience [ahirika]| and |moral recklessness::fearlessness of wrongdoing, lack of prudence [anottappa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two qualities.”

#### 2.185–189

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two qualities. What two? Non-anger and non-resentment ... |non-contempt::freedom from disparagement or ingratitude [amakkha]| and |non-feuding::freedom from rivalry or contention [apaḷāsa]| ... non-jealousy and non-stinginess ... |non-hypocrisy::sincerity, transparency, openness [amāyā]| and |non-treachery::honesty, freedom from deviousness [asāṭheyya]| ... |sense of right and wrong::sense of shame, conscience, modesty [hirī]| and |moral dread::fear of wrongdoing out of regard for others [ottappa]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two qualities.”

#### 2.190–194

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, one dwells with |suffering::discomfort, unpleasantness, discontentment, dissatisfaction, stress, pain, disease, i.e. mild or intense suffering [dukkha]|. What two? Anger and resentment ... contempt and feuding ... jealousy and stinginess ... hypocrisy and treachery ... lacking sense of right and wrong and moral recklessness. Endowed with these two qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| one dwells in suffering.”

#### 2.195–199

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, one dwells |in contentment::at ease, with happiness, in a pleasant abiding [sukha]|. What two? Non-anger and non-resentment ... non-contempt and non-feuding ... non-jealousy and non-stinginess ... non-hypocrisy and non-treachery ... sense of right and wrong and moral dread. Endowed with these two qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| one dwells in contentment.”

#### 2.200–204

“|Bhikkhus,::::| these two qualities lead to the |decline::regression, deterioration [parihāna]| of a |trainee::disciple in higher training who has not yet attained full awakening [sekha]| bhikkhu. What two? Anger and resentment ... contempt and feuding ... jealousy and stinginess ... hypocrisy and treachery ... lacking sense of right and wrong and moral recklessness. These two qualities|, bhikkhus,::::| lead to the decline of a trainee bhikkhu.”

#### 2.205–209

“|Bhikkhus,::::| these two qualities lead to the non-decline of a trainee bhikkhu. What two? Non-anger and non-resentment ... non-contempt and non-feuding ... non-jealousy and non-stinginess ... non-hypocrisy and non-treachery ... sense of right and wrong and moral dread. These two qualities|, bhikkhus,::::| lead to the non-decline of a trainee bhikkhu.”

#### 2.210–214

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, one is cast into |hell::a place of intense suffering; lit. no good fortune [niraya]| just as if carried and |placed::dropped, set down [nikkhitta]| there. What two? Anger and resentment ... contempt and feuding ... jealousy and stinginess ... hypocrisy and treachery ... lacking sense of right and wrong and moral recklessness. Endowed with these two qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| one is cast into hell just as if carried and placed there.”

#### 2.215–219

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, one is cast into |heaven::a blissful realm, celestial world [sagga]| just as if carried and placed there. What two? Non-anger and non-resentment ... non-contempt and non-feuding ... non-jealousy and non-stinginess ... non-hypocrisy and non-treachery ... sense of right and wrong and moral dread. Endowed with these two qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| one is cast into heaven just as if carried and placed there.”

#### 2.220–224

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, upon the breaking up of the body, after death, a certain person here is reborn in a state of loss, in a bad destination, in a |realm of downfall::realm of misery [vinipāta]|, in hell. What two? Anger and resentment ... contempt and feuding ... jealousy and stinginess ... hypocrisy and treachery ... lacking sense of right and wrong and moral recklessness. Endowed with these two qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| upon the breaking up of the body, after death, a certain person here is reborn in a state of loss, in a bad destination, in a realm of downfall, in hell.”

#### 2.225–229

“|Bhikkhus,::::| endowed with two qualities, upon the breaking up of the body, after death, a certain person here is reborn in a good destination, in a heavenly world. What two? Non-anger and non-resentment ... non-contempt and non-feuding ... non-jealousy and non-stinginess ... non-hypocrisy and non-treachery ... sense of right and wrong and moral dread. Endowed with these two qualities, |bhikkhus,::::| upon the breaking up of the body, after death, a certain person here is reborn in a good destination, in a heavenly world.”
