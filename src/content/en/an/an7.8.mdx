---
title: Saṁyojana sutta - Fetters
description: The Buddha lists the seven fetters that bind beings.
qualities: aversion, doubt, conceit, jealousy, stinginess
theme: principle
slug: an7.8
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these seven fetters. What seven?

The |fetter of attraction::bond of affection [anunayasaṁyojana]|, the |fetter of aversion::fetter of mental resistance, irritation, conflict [paṭighasaṁyojana]|, the |fetter of view::bond of opinions, conceptual beliefs [diṭṭhisaṁyojana]|, the fetter of doubt, the |fetter of conceit::chain of superiority [mānasaṁyojana]|, the fetter of passion for existence, and the fetter of ignorance.

These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the seven fetters.”
