---
title: Nidāna sutta - Causes
description: "The Buddha outlines six causes of kamma: unwholesome roots perpetuate to bad destinations, whereas wholesome roots foster good outcomes."
qualities: greed, aversion, delusion, giving, loving-kindness, radical attention
theme: wisdom
slug: an6.39
priority: 1.2
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these three causes for the arising of |kamma::action, deed, doing, volitional act [kamma]|. What three? 1.) |Greed::a grasping mental quality of craving, possessiveness, or lustful wanting that clings to objects or experiences; it fuels attachment and obstructs renunciation and contentment [lobha]| is a cause for the arising of kamma, 2.) |aversion::hatred, hostility, mental attitude of rejection, fault-finding, resentful disapproval [dosa]| is a cause for the arising of kamma, 3.) |delusion::illusion, misperception, erroneous belief, false idea, misapprehension; a fundamental distortion of reality that sustains confusion, clouds discernment, and fuels further doubt [moha]| is a cause for the arising of kamma. |Bhikkhus,::::| |non-greed::the absence of greed, lack of craving or lustful wanting; contentment, renunciation [alobha]| does not arise from greed; rather|, bhikkhus,::::| only greed arises from greed. |Bhikkhus,::::| |non-aversion::the absence of aversion, ill will, or hostility; loving-kindness, goodwill, friendliness [adosa]| does not arise from aversion; rather|, bhikkhus,::::| only aversion arises from aversion. |Bhikkhus,::::| |non-delusion::the absence of illusion, misapprehension, or distorted view; clear comprehension, understanding things as they really are [amoha]| does not arise from delusion; rather|, bhikkhus,::::| only delusion arises from delusion. |Bhikkhus,::::| through kamma arisen from greed, kamma arisen from aversion, or kamma arisen from delusion, |devas::celestial beings, deities, gods [deva]| are not manifested, humans are not manifested, nor any other |good destinations::fortunate realms of rebirth, happy destinations [sugati]|. Rather|, bhikkhus,::::| through kamma arisen from greed, kamma arisen from aversion, and kamma arisen from delusion, |hell::a place of intense suffering, lit. no good fortune [niraya]| is manifested, the animal realm is manifested, the |realm of afflicted spirits::the domain of ghosts or departed spirits [pettivisaya]| is manifested, or whatever other |bad destination::state of misery [duggati]|s there are. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the three causes for the arising of kamma.

|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these three causes for the arising of kamma. What three? 1.) Non-greed is a cause for the arising of kamma, 2.) non-aversion is a cause for the arising of kamma, 3.) non-delusion is a cause for the arising of kamma. |Bhikkhus,::::| greed does not arise from non-greed; rather|, bhikkhus,::::| only non-greed arises from non-greed. |Bhikkhus,::::| aversion does not arise from non-aversion; rather|, bhikkhus,::::| only non-aversion arises from non-aversion. |Bhikkhus,::::| delusion does not arise from non-delusion; rather|, bhikkhus,::::| only non-delusion arises from non-delusion. |Bhikkhus,::::| through kamma arisen from non-greed, kamma arisen from non-aversion, or kamma arisen from non-delusion, hell is not manifested, the animal realm is not manifested, the realm of afflicted spirits is not manifested, nor any other bad destinations. Rather|, bhikkhus,::::| through kamma arisen from non-greed, kamma arisen from non-aversion, and kamma arisen from non-delusion, devas are manifested, humans are manifested, and whatever other good destinations there are. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the three causes for the arising of kamma.”
