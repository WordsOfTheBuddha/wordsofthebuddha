#### 2.163

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Skill in meditative attainments::expertise in entering and abiding in the jhānas and formless attainments [samāpattikusalatā]| and |skill in emerging from meditative attainments::expertise in emerging from the jhānas and formless attainments at the determined time [samāpattivuṭṭhānakusalatā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.164

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Sincerity::honesty, straightness, integrity [ajjava]| and |tenderness::softness, mildness, flexibility [maddava]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.165

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Patience and |gentleness::meekness, kindness, mildness [soracca]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.166

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Friendliness::kindly speech, congeniality [sākhalya]| and hospitality. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.167

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Harmlessness and purity. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.168

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Not guarding the doors of the sense faculties and lack of moderation in eating. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.169

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Guarding the doors of the sense faculties and moderation in eating. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.170

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? The power of |reflection::careful consideration, examination [paṭisaṅkhāna]| and the power of |cultivation::meditative development [bhāvanā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.171

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? The power of mindfulness and the power of |collectedness::stability of mind, stillness of mind, mental composure [samādhi]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.172

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Tranquility::serenity, stilling, calming, mental unification [samatha]| and |penetrative vision::insight, seeing things distinctly as they are [vipassanā]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.173

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Failure in ethical conduct and failure in view. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.174

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Accomplishment in ethical conduct and accomplishment in view. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.175

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Purification of ethical conduct and purification of view. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.176

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? Purification of view and striving in accordance with one’s view. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.177

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Discontentment::not resting satisfied, refusal to plateau [asantuṭṭhitā]| with wholesome qualities and |unflagging perseverance::not turning back, unrelenting resolve [appaṭivānitā]| in striving. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.178

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Muddle-mindedness::forgetfulness, lack of mindfulness [muṭṭhassacca]| and |lack of clear awareness::inattentiveness, lack of comprehension [asampajañña]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”

#### 2.179

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these two things. What two? |Mindfulness::active presence of mind and lucid retention, recollection [sati]| and |clear awareness::full comprehension, deliberate attentiveness [sampajañña]|. These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the two things.”
