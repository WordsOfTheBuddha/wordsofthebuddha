**The Word of the Buddha**, compiled by Ñāṇatiloka Mahāthera, sets the teaching out in the Buddha’s own words and arranges it inside the four noble truths. The first edition appeared in German in 1906. The sections below follow the Buddhist Publication Society English edition, as one exposition aimed at deliverance from suffering.

It is a first map for someone new to the teachings, and a short summary for someone who already knows them. The passages are drawn from the five collections.

Each section below opens on its own page, in the order of the book. A citation opens that discourse at the paragraphs quoted there.

The reading opens with the threefold refuge and the five precepts.
