This is a site-curated anthology gathered from the discourses of the Pāli Canon whose teaching turns on **inquisitiveness** — asking, testing, and investigating. It is not a published book, but a reading path of graduated progress: it begins with the questions no one can opt out of — aging, illness, death, and loss — and then turns to how to question at all, which questions to set aside, how to investigate experience and the mind, how the training unfolds step by step, and what is finally seen for oneself.

The English translations to the discourses are provided by WordsOfTheBuddha.org project and based on the Pali texts of the connected discourses from the Mahāsaṅgīti Tipiṭaka Buddhavasse 2500 Pali Tipiṭaka in Roman script edition as made available by SuttaCentral.net.

### 1. The Questions We Avoid

1. [On Raṭṭhapāla - MN 82](/mn82) — a young man of means questions his way to ordination, then tells the king the four summaries of the Dhamma
2. [Unobtainable States - AN 5.48](/an5.48) — aging, illness, death, and loss: how the learned disciple meets what no one can prevent
3. [Kosala - AN 5.49](/an5.49) — a king grieves his queen; the Buddha contrasts self-torment with the practice that brings peace
4. [Subjects for Frequent Reflection - AN 5.57](/an5.57) — five subjects everyone shares, and one responsibility that is one's own
5. [Illness - AN 4.157](/an4.157) — rare even for a moment: freedom from mental illness, and how to train for it
6. [Ageing - Snp 4.6](/snp4.6) — all we call "mine" must be lost at death — the verse on not clinging

### 2. The Spirit of Inquiry

1. [The Noble Quest - MN 26](/mn26) — the Buddha recounts his own search, the model inquirer at work
2. [Don't Go by Authority Alone - AN 3.65](/an3.65) — the Kālāmas are told to test the teaching against their own experience
3. [Investigating the Teacher - MN 95](/mn95) — how to examine a teacher step by step, without blind faith or blind contempt
4. [Examining - MN 47](/mn47) — the Buddha himself lays out the method for investigating him: observe, question, and realize
5. [Person of Integrity - AN 4.73](/an4.73) — the qualities that distinguish a person of integrity from one lacking it
6. [Traits - AN 4.192](/an4.192) — a person's true character is known only over time, to a wise and attentive observer
7. [Fundamental Questions - AN 4.186](/an4.186) — a bhikkhu asks about the world, the mind, and wisdom, and receives a direct analysis

### 3. Questions and Answers

1. [At Kaṇṇakatthala - MN 90](/mn90) — King Pasenadi asks about omniscience, caste, and deities, and gets surprising answers
2. [The Bāhitika Cloth - MN 88](/mn88) — King Pasenadi asks Ānanda: is there any conduct of the Buddha the wise could criticize?
3. [To Prince Abhaya - MN 58](/mn58) — when does the Awakened One speak, and what makes speech worth hearing?
4. [To Māgaṇḍiya - MN 75](/mn75) — a critic calls sense restraint the destruction of spiritual growth; the Buddha dismantles the case
5. [The Shorter Series of Questions and Answers - MN 44](/mn44) — the layman Visākha and bhikkhunī Dhammadinnā trade subtle questions on identity, the path, and attainment
6. [Fetter - SN 41.1](/sn41.1) — elders debate what the "fetter" is; the layman Citta settles it with two yoked oxen
7. [The Brahmin Uṇṇābha - SN 48.42](/sn48.42) — the five senses converge in the mind, but where does the mind take recourse?
8. [The Greater Discourse on the Full Moon Night - MN 109](/mn109) — ten questions on the aggregates, and one very subtle correction
9. Encounters with Māra
    1. [Delights - SN 4.8](/sn4.8)
    2. [Kingship - SN 4.20](/sn4.20)

### 4. Questions the Buddha Set Aside
