“I hope that from living constantly together,
you do not look down upon the wise one?
Is the |torch-bearer::bearer of the torch of wisdom [ukkādhāra]| of mankind
revered by you?”

“Not from living constantly together
do I look down upon the wise one;
the torch-bearer of mankind
is always revered by me.”

“Having left the |five cords of sensual pleasure::the pleasant objects of the five physical senses [pañca kāmaguṇa]|,
so pleasing and delightful;
having gone forth from home in |faith::confidence, conviction, devotion, trust [saddhā]|,
be one who makes an end of suffering!

Associate with |good friends::noble friends who support one on the spiritual path [kalyāṇamittā]|,
and with remote lodgings,
secluded and quiet;
|be moderate::know the correct measure [mattaññū]| in eating.

For robes and almsfood,
medicinal requisites and lodgings:
do not have craving for these;
do not return to the world again.

Restrained in the |Pātimokkha::monastic code of discipline which promotes harmonious and pure conduct with others [pātimokkha]|,
and in the five sense faculties;
may |mindfulness directed to the body::awareness immersed in the body, dwelling in the body [kāyagatāsati]| be yours,
be full of |disenchantment::de-illusionment, disinterest, dispassion [nibbidā]|.

Shun the |sign of the attractive::an attractive object that is the basis for lust [subhanimitta]|,
which provokes lust;
cultivate the mind on the |unattractive::foulness; lit. not beautiful [asubha]|,
unified and |well composed::completely calm, totally settled, attentive, completely collected [susamāhita]|.

Cultivate the |signless::featureless, free of mental images, unconditioned [animitta]|,
and give up the |underlying tendency to conceit::latent tendency toward pride and self-comparison [mānānusaya]|;
then, |through complete comprehension of conceit::through full understanding of pride, egotism, superiority, comparing oneself [mānābhisamaya]|,
you will fare on |cooled::at peace [upasanta]|.”

In this way the Blessed One frequently instructed the venerable Rāhula with these verses.
