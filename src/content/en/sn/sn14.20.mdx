---
title: Anottappamūlaka sutta - Beginning With Morally Reckless
description: Beginning with moral recklessness, the Buddha reveals how those who lack dread of wrongdoing band together in ignorance, whereas those endowed with prudence and wisdom unite in noble fellowship.
qualities: moral recklessness, fear of wrongdoing, learning, laziness, rousing of energy, muddle-mindedness, mindfulness, lack of discernment, wisdom
theme: principle, cultivating discernment, wisdom
slug: sn14.20
---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. The |morally reckless::fearlessness of wrongdoing, lack of prudence [anottappa]| come together and associate with the morally reckless; those with |little learning::ignorant, uneducated [appassuta]| come together and associate with those with little learning; the |undiscerning::without wisdom; lit. bad understanding [duppañña]| come together and associate with the undiscerning; those with |fear of wrongdoing::with moral dread, respect for others [ottappa]| come together and associate with those with fear of wrongdoing; the |very learned::well educated, knowledgeable [bahussuta]| come together and associate with the very learned; the |wise::discerning, percipient; lit. having the wisdom quality [paññavant]| come together and associate with the wise.

The morally reckless come together and associate with the morally reckless; the |lazy::procrastinating, inactive person, indolent [kusīta]| come together and associate with the lazy; the undiscerning come together and associate with the undiscerning; those with fear of wrongdoing come together and associate with those with fear of wrongdoing; those |with energy aroused::with initiative [āraddhavīriya]| come together and associate with those with energy aroused; the wise come together and associate with the wise.

The morally reckless come together and associate with the morally reckless; the |muddle-minded::forgetful, not mindful [muṭṭhassatī]| come together and associate with the muddle-minded; the undiscerning come together and associate with the undiscerning; those with fear of wrongdoing come together and associate with those with fear of wrongdoing; those |attending mindfully::with presence of mind [upaṭṭhitassatī]| come together and associate with those attending mindfully; the wise come together and associate with the wise.”
