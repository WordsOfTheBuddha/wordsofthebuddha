Thus have I heard—At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling at Sāvatthi, in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s Park.

Then, when the night had advanced, a certain deity, radiant in appearance and illuminating the entire Jeta’s Grove, approached the Blessed One. Having drawn near and paid homage to the Blessed One, she stood to one side. Standing to one side, that deity recited these verses in the presence of the Blessed One:

“When a house is ablaze,
the vessel that is carried out
is the one that is of use,
not the one left inside to burn.

So since the world is ablaze
with old age and death,
one should carry out what one can by |giving::the act of giving or donating, with an intention to give [dāna]|,
for what is given is |well saved::lit. well carried away [sunīhata]|.

What is given has happiness as its fruit,
not so what is not given;
thieves take it, or kings,
fire burns it, or it is lost.

Then in the end one leaves behind
the body along with all possessions;
knowing this, a wise person
should enjoy and also give;
having given and enjoyed according to their means,
blameless, they go to a heavenly world.”
