At Sāvatthi.

#### Inferior Inclinations

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. Those |of inferior inclinations::with inferior dispositions, with low tendencies [hīnādhimuttikā]| come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations. In the past too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings came together and associated by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations came together and associated with those of inferior inclinations.

In the future too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings will come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations will come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations.

Even now, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations.

|Bhikkhus,::::| just as |excrement::dung, faeces [gūtha]| comes together and fits with excrement; urine comes together and fits with urine; |saliva::spit [kheḷa]| comes together and fits with saliva; |pus::suppuration [pubba]| comes together and fits with pus; blood comes together and fits with blood; so too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations. In the past too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings came together and associated by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations came together and associated with those of inferior inclinations. In the future too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings will come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations will come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations. Even now, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations.

#### Noble Inclinations

|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those |of noble inclinations::with ethical inclinations [kalyāṇādhimuttika]| come together and associate with those of noble inclinations. In the past too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings came together and associated by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations came together and associated with those of noble inclinations.

In the future too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings will come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations will come together and associate with those of noble inclinations. Even now, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.

|Bhikkhus,::::| just as milk comes together and mixes with milk; oil with oil; |ghee::clarified butter [sappi]| with ghee; honey with honey; and |jaggery::molasses [phāṇita]| with jaggery; so too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations come together and associate with those of noble inclinations. In the past too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings came together and associated by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations came together and associated with those of noble inclinations. In the future too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings will come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations will come together and associate with those of noble inclinations. Even now, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of noble inclinations come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.

The Blessed One said this. Having spoken thus, the Accomplished One further said this:

#### Verse

“From |association::mingling, mixing, contact, intimacy, touch, entanglement [saṃsagga]|, |craving::entanglements, subtle attachments, desire [vanatha]| arises,
through non-association, it |is cut-off::is severed, ceases to exist, is broken [chijjati]|;
like one who boards a fragile raft,
and sinks in the mighty ocean.

So by consorting with a |lazy person::a procrastinating, inactive person, indolent [kusīta]|,
even a virtuous person sinks;
therefore, one should |steer clear of::avoid, shun [parivajjati]| a lazy person,
who is |weak in effort::low in energy, lacking in endurance [hīnavīriya]|.

|Dwell together::keep company [sahāvasa]| with the wise,
with the |Noble Ones::distinguished, an ethically noble person, an awakened being [ariya]| who dwell |in seclusion::separated, withdrawn, detached [pavivitta]|;
with |meditators::yogis [jhāyi]| who are |resolute::determined, intent [pahitatta]| on their goal,
who are |continuously::regularly, reliably [nicca]| |with energy aroused::with initiative [āraddhavīriya]|.”
