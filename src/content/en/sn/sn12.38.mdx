---
title: Cetanā sutta - Intending
description: Intention, planning, and underlying tendencies provide the foundation for consciousness to fuel future rebirth. When all three cease, consciousness remains unestablished, bringing the complete cessation of suffering.
qualities: suffering, ending, right intention, wrong intention, consciousness
theme: wisdom, cultivating discernment
slug: sn12.38
priority: 1.4
---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| here, whatever one |intends::wills, thinks about [ceteti]|, what one |plans::designs, imagines [pakappeti]|, and whatever one |has a tendency towards::is pre-occupied with, is attached to; lit. lies alongside [anuseti]|, |bhikkhus,::::| this becomes a |basis::foundation, support [ārammaṇa]| |for the continuity::lit. for standing [ṭhitiyā]| of |consciousness::that dependently arisen knowing which, when rooted in ignorance and supported by intentional constructs, finds a footing and becomes established in a sense realm, a form realm, or a formless realm leading to production of renewed existence in the future [viññāṇa]|. When there is a basis, there is the |establishing::lit. standing back [patiṭṭhanta]| of consciousness. When consciousness is |established::lit. stood back [patiṭṭhita]| and grows, there is the production of renewed existence in future. When there is the production of renewed existence in future, future birth, aging and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure, and despair come to be. Thus is the arising of this entire mass of |suffering::discomfort, unpleasantness, discontentment, dissatisfaction, stress, pain, disease, i.e. mild or intense suffering [dukkha]|.

If|, bhikkhus,::::| one does not intend and does not plan, but still has an underlying tendency, this becomes a basis for the establishing of consciousness. When there is a basis, there is the establishing of consciousness. When consciousness is established and grows, there is the production of renewed existence in future. When there is the production of renewed existence in future, future birth, aging and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure, and despair come to be. Thus is the arising of this entire heap of suffering.

However, |bhikkhus,::::| when one neither intends, nor plans, nor has any underlying tendency, there is no basis for the continuity of consciousness. With no basis, there is no support for the continuity of consciousness. When consciousness |is not established::remains unestablished; lit. not stood back [appatiṭṭhita]| and does not come to growth, there is no production of renewed existence in future. When there is no production of renewed existence in future, future birth, aging and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure, and despair do not come to be. Thus there is the |cessation::ending, termination [nirodha]| of this entire heap of suffering.”
