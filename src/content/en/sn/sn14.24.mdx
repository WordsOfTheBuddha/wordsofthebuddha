---
title: Dussīla sutta - Unprincipled
description: Contrasting ethical failure with virtue, the Buddha demonstrates that the unprincipled inevitably band together with the unprincipled, whereas the virtuous and conscientious gather around wise and noble companions.
qualities: faithlessness, faith, shamelessness, conscience, moral recklessness, fear of wrongdoing, unprincipled conduct, ethical conduct, lack of discernment, wisdom
theme: principle, cultivating discernment, wisdom
slug: sn14.24
---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. The |faithless::without confidence, without conviction [assaddhā]| come together and associate with the faithless; the |unashamed::shameless, without conscience [ahirikā]| come together and associate with the unashamed; the |morally reckless::fearlessness of wrongdoing, lack of prudence [anottappa]| come together and associate with the morally reckless; the |unprincipled::without regard for ethical conduct [dussīlā]| come together and associate with the unprincipled; the |undiscerning::without wisdom; lit. bad understanding [duppaññā]| come together and associate with the undiscerning.

The |faithful::confident, believing, devoted, trusting [saddhā]| come together and associate with the faithful; the |conscientious::honorable [hirimana]| come together and associate with the conscientious; those with |fear of wrongdoing::with moral dread, respect for others [ottappa]| come together and associate with those with fear of wrongdoing; the |virtuous::ethical, moral [sīlavant]| come together and associate with the virtuous; the |wise::discerning, percipient; lit. having the wisdom quality [paññavanto]| come together and associate with the wise.”
