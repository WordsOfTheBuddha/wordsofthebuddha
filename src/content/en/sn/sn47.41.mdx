At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| dwell with |minds well established::with the mind firmly planted, well grounded [suppatiṭṭhitacitta]| in the |four establishments of mindfulness::four foundations or objective domains of mindfulness that one gradually establishes and cultivates as the direct path of practice [cattāro + satipaṭṭhāna]|. Do not let the |deathless::deathless state, epithet of Nibbāna [amata]| be |lost to you::perish, go to waste [panassati]|. In which four? 1.) Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu dwells observing the body in and of itself, |with continuous effort::ardent, zealous, with energy, with application; lit. burning [ātāpī]|, |clearly aware::with attentiveness, with clear and full comprehension, intentional, deliberate, purposeful [sampajāna]| and with excellent recollection, having removed |craving and distress::greediness and dissatisfaction, wanting and unhappiness, craving and aversion [abhijjhā + domanassa]| with regard to the world; 2.) |felt experience::pleasant, neutral, or painful sensation, feeling felt on contact through eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind; second of the five aggregates [vedanā]| <collapse>in and of itself, with continuous effort, clearly aware and with excellent recollection, having removed craving and distress with regard to the world;</collapse> 3.) |mind::mindset, disposition; the affective state of awareness experiencing the present moment [citta]| <collapse>in and of itself, with continuous effort, clearly aware and with excellent recollection, having removed craving and distress with regard to the world;</collapse> 4.) |mental qualities::characteristics, traits, and tendencies of the mind, shaped by repeated actions and sustained attention, guided by particular ways of understanding; they may be wholesome or unwholesome, bright or dark [dhammā]| in and of themselves, with continuous effort, clearly aware and with excellent recollection, having removed craving and distress with regard to the world. In these|, bhikkhus,::::| four establishments of mindfulness, dwell with minds well established. Do not let the deathless be lost to you.”
