At Sāvatthi.

Then the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī, having dressed early in the morning and taking her alms bowl and outer robe, entered Sāvatthi for alms. Having wandered for alms in Sāvatthi, after her meal and having returned from her alms round, she approached the |Dark Wood::name of a wood outside Sāvatthi; lit. dark wood [andhavana]| for the day’s abiding. Having entered the Dark Wood, she sat down at the foot of a certain tree for the day’s abiding.

Then |Māra::the ruler of the sensual realm, often depicted as a tempter who tries to obstruct beings from the path to liberation [māra]| the Evil One, wishing to arouse fear, trepidation, and terror in the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī, wishing to make her scatter her |collectedness::stability of mind, unshakeable mind, samādhi [samādhi]|, approached the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī; having approached, he addressed the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī in verse:

“Why do you sit alone with a tearful face,
as though your child has died?
Having entered the middle of the woods alone,
are you seeking a man?”

Then it occurred to the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī: “Now who is this, a human or a non-human, that speaks this verse?”

Then it occurred to the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī: “This is Māra the Evil One, who speaks this verse wishing to arouse fear, trepidation, and terror in me, wishing to make me scatter my collectedness.”

Then the bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī, having understood, “This is Māra the Evil One,” replied to Māra the Evil One in verses:

“I have gone |beyond::past, completely beyond [accanta]| the death of sons;
with this, the search for men has ended.
I do not sorrow, I do not weep,
nor do I fear you, friend.

Destroyed is |delight::pleasure, enjoyment, relish [nandi]| in every respect,
the |mass of darkness::heap of ignorance [tamokkhandha]| has been pierced;
having conquered the army of Death,
I dwell |taintless::undefiled, free from effluents, free from mental defilements [anāsava]|.”

Then Māra the Evil One, thinking, “The bhikkhunī Kisāgotamī knows me,” dejected and disappointed, disappeared right there.
