---
title: Cakkhu sutta - Eye
description: The arising of the six internal sense bases brings suffering and aging; their complete cessation is the cessation of suffering.
qualities: suffering, ending
theme: principle
slug: sn26.1
---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| the arising, continuation, production, and manifestation of the eye is the arising of suffering, the continuation of disease, the manifestation of aging and death. The arising, continuation, production, and manifestation of the ear ... of the nose ... of the tongue ... of the body ... of the mind is the arising of suffering, the continuation of disease, the manifestation of aging and death.

And|, bhikkhus,::::| the |cessation::ending, termination [nirodha]|, |settling::calming, conciliation, subsiding [vūpasama]|, and |passing away::disappearance, vanishing, settling down [atthaṅgama]| of the eye is the cessation of suffering, the settling of disease, the passing away of aging and death. The cessation, settling, and passing away of the ear ... of the nose ... of the tongue ... of the body ... of the mind is the cessation of suffering, the settling of disease, the passing away of aging and death.”
