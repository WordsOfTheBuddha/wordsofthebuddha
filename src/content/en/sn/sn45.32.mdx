At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| I will teach you the one |practicing wrongly::going the wrong way [micchāpaṭipanna]| and the one |practicing rightly::going the right way [sammāpaṭipanna]|. Listen to this.

And who|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one practicing wrongly? Here, bhikkhus, someone has wrong view ... wrong collectedness. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is called the one practicing wrongly.

And who|, bhikkhus,::::| is the one practicing rightly? Here, bhikkhus, someone has right view ... right collectedness. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is called the one practicing rightly.”
