“What |overcomes::overpowers, overwhelms [addhabhavi]| everything?
What is it that nothing excels?
What is the one thing
that has everything under its sway?”

[The Blessed One]: “|Name::feeling, perception, intention, contact and attention — the elementary acts of recognition and designation — are "name" [nāma]| overcomes everything.
Nothing excels name.
Name is the one thing
that has everything under its sway.”
