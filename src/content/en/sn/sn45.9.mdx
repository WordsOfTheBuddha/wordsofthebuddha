title: Sūka sutta - Spike
description: Just as only a well-directed spike of rice can pierce the hand, only a bhikkhu with a well-directed view and path-cultivation can pierce ignorance, arouse true knowledge, and realize Nibbāna through cultivating the noble eightfold path.
qualities: wrong view, right view, ignorance, true knowledge, cultivation
theme: wisdom
slug: sn45.9
simile: spike of rice or barley

---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| suppose there is a misdirected spike of rice or barley, and it is pressed by hand or foot. It is impossible for it to pierce the hand or foot and draw blood. Why is that? Because the spike is misdirected|, bhikkhus::::|.

In the same way, |bhikkhus,::::| it is impossible for a bhikkhu with misdirected view and misdirected path-cultivation to pierce |ignorance::fundamental unawareness or misunderstanding of the true nature of reality, not experientially understanding the four noble truths [avijjā]|, arouse |true knowledge::wisdom, the direct realization of truth [vijjā]|, and realize |Nibbāna::complete cooling, letting go of everything, deathless, freedom from calamity, the non-disintegrating; lit. blowing away [nibbāna]|. Why is that? Because the view is misdirected|, bhikkhus::::|.

Suppose|, bhikkhus,::::| there is a well-directed spike of rice or barley, and it is pressed by hand or foot. It is possible for it to pierce the hand or foot and draw blood. Why is that? Because the spike is well-directed|, bhikkhus::::|.

In the same way, |bhikkhus,::::| it is possible for a bhikkhu with well-directed view and well-directed path-cultivation to pierce ignorance, arouse true knowledge, and realize Nibbāna. Why is that? Because the view is well-directed|, bhikkhus::::|.

And how|, bhikkhus,::::| does a bhikkhu with well-directed view and well-directed path-cultivation pierce ignorance, arouse true knowledge, and realize Nibbāna? Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu cultivates right view, which is |supported by seclusion::dependent on detachment, by means of disengagement [vivekanissita]|, |supported by dispassion::based on fading of desire [virāganissita]|, |supported by cessation::based on ending [nirodhanissita]|, and |culminating in complete relinquishment::ripens in release, culminating in letting go [vossaggapariṇāmī]| ... cultivates right collectedness, which is supported by seclusion, supported by dispassion, supported by cessation, and culminating in complete relinquishment. It is in this way|, bhikkhus,::::| that a bhikkhu with well-directed view and well-directed path-cultivation pierces ignorance, arouses true knowledge, and realizes Nibbāna.”
