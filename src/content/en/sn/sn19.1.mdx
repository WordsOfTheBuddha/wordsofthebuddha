Thus have I heard—At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling at |Rājagaha::name of a city; capital of Magadha; lit. king’s house [rājagaha]|, in the Bamboo Grove, the Squirrels’ feeding ground.

Now at that time, the venerable Lakkhaṇa and the venerable |Mahāmoggallāna::foremost disciple of the Buddha in psychic power; lit. Moggallāna the great [mahāmoggallāna]| were dwelling on |Vulture’s Peak::name of a mountain peak around Rājagaha; lit. vulture’s peak [gijjhakūṭa]| Mountain. Then the venerable Mahāmoggallāna, having dressed early in the morning, taking his alms bowl and outer robe, approached where the venerable Lakkhaṇa was. Having approached, he said to the venerable Lakkhaṇa: “Come, friend Lakkhaṇa, let us enter Rājagaha for alms.”

“Yes, friend,” the venerable Lakkhaṇa replied to the venerable Mahāmoggallāna.

Then, as the venerable Mahāmoggallāna was descending from Vulture’s Peak Mountain, he displayed a smile at a certain spot. Then the venerable Lakkhaṇa said to the venerable Mahāmoggallāna: “What is the cause, friend Moggallāna, what is the reason for displaying a smile?”

“Friend Lakkhaṇa, it is not the time for this question. Ask me this question in the presence of the Blessed One.”

Then the venerable Lakkhaṇa and the venerable Mahāmoggallāna, having walked for alms in Rājagaha and returned from their alms round after their meal, approached the Blessed One. Having approached, they paid homage to the Blessed One and sat down to one side. Sitting to one side, the venerable Lakkhaṇa said to the venerable Mahāmoggallāna:

“Just now, as the venerable Mahāmoggallāna was descending from Vulture’s Peak Mountain, he displayed a smile at a certain spot. What is the cause, friend Moggallāna, what is the reason for displaying a smile?”

“Just now, friend, as I was descending from Vulture’s Peak Mountain, I saw a skeleton moving through the air. Vultures, crows, and hawks were pursuing it again and again, pecking between its ribs, tearing at it, and ripping it apart, while it uttered cries of pain. Then, friend, it occurred to me: ‘It is unbelievable! It is astonishing! That there could be such a being, that there could be such a spirit, that there could be such an |individual form::acquisition of personal existence [attabhāvapaṭilābha]|!’”

Then the Blessed One addressed the bhikkhus:

“Truly, |bhikkhus,::::| the disciples dwell having become vision; truly, |bhikkhus,::::| the disciples dwell having become knowledge, in that a disciple will know, see, or bear witness to such a thing.

Formerly, |bhikkhus,::::| I too saw that being, but I did not speak of it. For if I had spoken of it, others would not have believed me; and for those who did not believe me, it would lead to their harm and suffering for a long time.

That being|, bhikkhus,::::| was a cattle butcher in this very Rājagaha. As a result of that kamma, having boiled in hell for many years, many hundreds of years, many thousands of years, many hundreds of thousands of years, through the residue of that same kamma’s result, he experiences such an individual form.”
