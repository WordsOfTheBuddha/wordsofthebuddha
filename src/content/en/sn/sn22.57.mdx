At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu who is skilled in seven cases and an investigator in three ways is called in this |Dhamma::teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [dhamma]| and |Vinaya::code of monastic discipline rules, training [vinaya]| a |perfected one::consummate one [kevalī]|, |one who has fulfilled one’s training::one who has lived the spiritual life [vusitavant]|, the supreme person.

And how|, bhikkhus,::::| is a bhikkhu skilled in seven cases? Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu |discerns::distinguishes, understands, knows clearly [pajānāti]| form, discerns the arising of form, discerns the cessation of form, discerns the way of practice leading to the cessation of form; discerns the |gratification::satisfaction, pleasure, enjoyment, sweetness [assāda]| in form, discerns the |drawback::disadvantage, unsatisfactoriness, inadequacy, danger [ādīnava]| in form, discerns the |escape::way out, remedy [nissaraṇa]| in the case of form; discerns feeling ... perception ... intentional constructs ... discerns consciousness, discerns the arising of consciousness, discerns the cessation of consciousness, discerns the way of practice leading to the cessation of consciousness; discerns the gratification in consciousness, discerns the drawback in consciousness, discerns the escape in the case of consciousness.

And what|, bhikkhus,::::| is form? The four great elements and the form derived from the four great elements. This is called form|, bhikkhus::::|. With the arising of |sustenance::fuel, nourishment [āhāra]| comes the arising of form; with the cessation of sustenance comes the cessation of form. Just this Noble Eightfold Path is the way of practice leading to the cessation of form, that is — |right view::view that is in line with the Dhamma - teachings of the Buddha that point to the nature of reality, the ultimate truth [sammādiṭṭhi]|, <collapse>right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness,</collapse> and |right collectedness::correct mental composure that stabilizes the mind and supports clarity of thoughts [sammāsamādhi]|.

The happiness and |pleasure::a positive state of mind; agreeable feeling born of mental contact, mental happiness [somanassa]| that arise dependent on form—this is the gratification in form. The fact that form is |impermanent::unstable, transient, unreliable [anicca]|, |unsatisfactory::uncomfortable, unpleasant [dukkha]|, and |subject to change::of the nature of alteration, decay [vipariṇāmadhamma]|—this is the drawback in form. The removal and abandonment of |desire::intention, wish, impulse, interest [chanda]| and |passion::intense desire, strong emotion, infatuation, obsession, lust [rāga]| for form—this is the escape in the case of form.

For any ascetics or brahmins|, bhikkhus,::::| who—having |directly known::completely known, realized [abhiññāya]| form thus, having directly known the arising of form thus, having directly known the cessation of form thus, having directly known the way of practice leading to the cessation of form thus; having directly known the gratification in form thus, having directly known the drawback in form thus, having directly known the escape in the case of form thus—are practicing for |disenchantment::de-illusionment, disinterest, dispassion [nibbidā]|, |fading of desire::dispassion, detachment [virāga]|, and the cessation of form, they are practicing well. Those who are practicing well have |found a footing::found a support [gādhati]| in this Dhamma and Vinaya.

And any ascetics or brahmins|, bhikkhus,::::| who—having directly known form thus, having directly known the arising of form thus, having directly known the cessation of form thus, having directly known the way of practice leading to the cessation of form thus; having directly known the gratification in form thus, having directly known the drawback in form thus, having directly known the escape in the case of form thus—are freed |without clinging::without grasping [anupādā]| through disenchantment, fading of desire, and the cessation of form, they are well freed. Those who are well freed are perfected ones. For the perfected ones, there is no cycle to describe.

And what|, bhikkhus,::::| is feeling? There are these six classes of feeling|, bhikkhus::::|: feeling born of eye-contact, feeling born of ear-contact, feeling born of nose-contact, feeling born of tongue-contact, feeling born of body-contact, feeling born of mind-contact. This is called feeling|, bhikkhus::::|. With the arising of |contact::sense impingement, raw experience, touch [phassa]| comes the arising of feeling; with the cessation of contact comes the cessation of feeling. Just this Noble Eightfold Path is the way of practice leading to the cessation of feeling, that is—right view, <collapse>right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness,</collapse> and right collectedness.

The happiness and pleasure that arise dependent on feeling—this is the gratification in feeling. The fact that feeling is impermanent, unsatisfactory, and subject to change—this is the drawback in feeling. The removal and abandonment of desire and passion for feeling—this is the escape in the case of feeling.

For any ascetics or brahmins|, bhikkhus,::::| who—having directly known feeling thus, having directly known the arising of feeling thus, having directly known the cessation of feeling thus, having directly known the way of practice leading to the cessation of feeling thus; having directly known the gratification in feeling thus, having directly known the drawback in feeling thus, having directly known the escape in the case of feeling thus—are practicing for disenchantment, fading of desire, and the cessation of feeling, they are practicing well. Those who are practicing well have found a footing in this Dhamma and Vinaya.

And any ascetics or brahmins|, bhikkhus,::::| who—having directly known feeling thus, <collapse>having directly known the arising of feeling thus, having directly known the cessation of feeling thus, having directly known the way of practice leading to the cessation of feeling thus; having directly known the gratification in feeling thus, having directly known the drawback in feeling thus, having directly known the escape in the case of feeling thus—are freed without clinging through disenchantment, fading of desire, and the cessation of feeling, they are well freed. And what|, bhikkhus,::::| is consciousness? There are these six classes of consciousness|, bhikkhus::::|: eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, mind-consciousness. This is called consciousness|, bhikkhus::::|. With the arising of |name and form::The mutually dependent pair of mental designation and material perception: "Name" consists of feeling, perception, intention, contact, and attention; "Form" consists of the four great primaries (earth, water, fire, wind) and derived form, experienced as perception of form through sensory resistance [nāmarūpa]| comes the arising of consciousness; with the cessation of name and form comes the cessation of consciousness. |Bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu who is skilled in seven cases and an investigator in three ways is called in this Dhamma and Vinaya a perfected one, one who has fulfilled one’s training, the supreme person.”
