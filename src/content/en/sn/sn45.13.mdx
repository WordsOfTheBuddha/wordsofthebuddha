At Sāvatthi.

Then, a certain bhikkhu approached where the Blessed One was; having approached and paid homage, he sat down to one side. Sitting to one side, that bhikkhu said to the Blessed One: “Venerable sir, it is said ‘a trainee, a trainee.’ In what way, venerable sir, is one a trainee?”

“Here, |bhikkhu,::::| one is endowed with a trainee’s right view, <collapse>one is endowed with a trainee’s right intention, one is endowed with a trainee’s right speech, one is endowed with a trainee’s right action, one is endowed with a trainee’s right livelihood, one is endowed with a trainee’s right effort, one is endowed with a trainee’s right mindfulness,</collapse> one is endowed with a trainee’s right collectedness. This is how one is a trainee.”
