“By what is the world led astray?
By what is it carried away?
What is the one thing
that has everything under its sway?”

“The world is led astray by |mind::mindset, disposition; the affective state of awareness experiencing the present moment [citta]|.
By mind it is carried away.
Mind is the one thing
that has everything under its sway.”
