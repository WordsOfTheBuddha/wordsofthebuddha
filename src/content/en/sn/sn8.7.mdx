title: Pavāraṇā sutta - The Invitation
description: On the full moon night of the Pavāraṇā ceremony, the Buddha invites the assembly of five hundred arahant bhikkhus to reproach him for any bodily or verbal fault. Sāriputta declares that the Buddha is blameless, the Buddha extols Sāriputta’s great wisdom, and Venerable Vaṅgīsa utters verses in praise of the pure assembly and the Buddha.
qualities: ethical conduct, wisdom, craving
theme: inspiration

  - Venerable Vaṅgīsa

At one time, the Blessed One was dwelling at Sāvatthi, in the Eastern Park, at |Migāramātā’s mansion::name of a monastery outside Sāvatthi, built by Visākhā; lit. Migāra’s Mother’s Hall [migāramātupāsāda]|, with a large assembly of bhikkhus—about five hundred bhikkhus, all of them arahants. Now on that occasion, the Blessed One was seated in the open air, surrounded by the |Saṅgha::The community of monks and nuns practicing in line with the Buddha’s teachings. In the broader sense, this is the community of disciples who have realized the noble path and fruition through the Buddha’s teachings [saṅgha]| of bhikkhus on the |Uposatha::Occurring on specific lunar days such as the full moon, new moon, and quarter moons, this is a time for renewing virtue, deepening practice, and purifying the mind. [uposatha]| day of the fifteenth, for the |Pavāraṇā ceremony::invitation ceremony at the end of the rains retreat [pavāraṇā]|.

Then, surveying the silent Saṅgha of bhikkhus, the Blessed One addressed the bhikkhus: “Come now, |bhikkhus,::::| I invite you. Do you reproach me in anything, whether bodily or verbal?”

When this was said, the venerable Sāriputta rose from his seat, draped his outer robe over one shoulder, raised his joined palms towards the Blessed One, and said to the Blessed One: “We do not reproach the Blessed One in anything, venerable sir, whether bodily or verbal. For the Blessed One, venerable sir, is the originator of the path unarisen before, the producer of the path unproduced before, the declarer of the path undeclared before; he is the knower of the path, the discoverer of the path, the one skilled in the path. And now, venerable sir, his disciples dwell following that path and become possessed of it afterwards. And I, venerable sir, invite the Blessed One: does the Blessed One reproach me in anything, whether bodily or verbal?”

“I do not reproach you in anything, Sāriputta, whether bodily or verbal. You are |wise::astute, intelligent, learned, skilled [paṇḍita]|, Sāriputta; you are |of great wisdom::very wise, extremely intelligent [mahāpañña]|, Sāriputta; you have widespread wisdom, Sāriputta; you have |joyful wisdom::playful understanding, intelligent sense of humor; lit. laughing wisdom [hāsapañña]|, Sāriputta; you have swift wisdom, Sāriputta; you have piercing wisdom, Sāriputta; you have penetrative wisdom, Sāriputta. Just as, Sāriputta, the eldest son of a |wheel-turning monarch::a just and virtuous ruler whose domain extends over the entire known world [rājā cakkavattī]| rightly keeps rolling the wheel set into motion by his father; even so, Sāriputta, you rightly keep rolling the |unsurpassed::highest, incomparable [anuttara]| wheel of the Dhamma set into motion by me.”

“Since, venerable sir, the Blessed One does not reproach me in anything, whether bodily or verbal: does the Blessed One reproach these five hundred bhikkhus in anything, whether bodily or verbal?”

“I do not reproach these five hundred bhikkhus either, Sāriputta, in anything, whether bodily or verbal. For of these five hundred bhikkhus, Sāriputta, sixty bhikkhus possess the threefold knowledge, sixty bhikkhus possess the six direct knowledges, sixty bhikkhus are |liberated in both ways::emancipated in two regards [ubhatobhāgavimutta]|, and the rest are |liberated by wisdom::emancipated by insight [paññāvimutta]|.”

Then the venerable Vaṅgīsa rose from his seat, draped his outer robe over one shoulder, raised his joined palms towards the Blessed One, and said to the Blessed One: “An inspiration has come to me, Blessed One! An inspiration has come to me, Accomplished One!”

“Then express your inspiration, Vaṅgīsa,” said the Blessed One.

Then the venerable Vaṅgīsa extolled the Blessed One in his presence with suitable verses:

“Today, on the fifteenth day of purification,
five hundred bhikkhus have assembled;
they have severed the bonds of the fetters,
|untroubled::undisturbed, calm; lit. free from trembling [anīgha]| |seers::sage, hermit, monk [isi]| with rebirth ended.

Just as a wheel-turning monarch,
surrounded by his ministers,
travels all around
this earth bounded by the ocean;

Even so, disciples attend upon
the victor in battle, the |unsurpassed::highest, incomparable [anuttara]| caravan leader,
possessors of the threefold knowledge,
|who have gone beyond death::who have transcended mortality [maccuhāyino]|.

All are sons of the Blessed One,
no |chaff::waste material, empty husk [palāpa]| is found here;
I pay homage to the |Kinsman of the Sun::an epithet of the Buddha; lit. relative of the sun [ādiccabandhu]|,
the remover of the dart of craving.”
