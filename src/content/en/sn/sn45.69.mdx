---
title: Yonisomanasikārasampadā sutta - Accomplishment in Radical Attention
description: The Buddha teaches that accomplishment in radical attention is the foundation for cultivating the noble eightfold path.
qualities: radical attention, cultivation, giving up
theme: wisdom, inspiration
priority: 1.1
slug: sn45.69
---

“That is—accomplishment in radical attention. For a bhikkhu accomplished in radical attention, |bhikkhus,::::| this is to be expected: they will cultivate the noble eightfold path, they will frequently practice the noble eightfold path.

And how|, bhikkhus,::::| does a bhikkhu accomplished in radical attention cultivate the noble eightfold path, frequently practice the noble eightfold path? Here, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu cultivates right view, which is |supported by seclusion::dependent on detachment, by means of disengagement [vivekanissita]|, |supported by dispassion::based on fading of desire [virāganissita]|, |supported by cessation::based on ending [nirodhanissita]|, and |culminating in complete relinquishment::ripens in release, culminating in letting go [vossaggapariṇāmī]| ... cultivates right collectedness, which is supported by seclusion, supported by dispassion, supported by cessation, and culminating in complete relinquishment. It is in this way|, bhikkhus,::::| that a bhikkhu accomplished in radical attention cultivates and frequently practices the noble eightfold path.”
