---
title: Hīnādhimuttika sutta - With Inferior Inclinations
description: Beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations come together and associate with others of similar inferior inclinations.
qualities: good friendship, bad friendship, companionship
theme: principle, wisdom
slug: sn14.14
---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. Those |of inferior inclinations::with inferior dispositions, with low tendencies [hīnādhimuttikā]| come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations; those |of noble inclinations::with ethical inclinations [kalyāṇādhimuttika]| come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.

In the past too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings came together and associated by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations came together and associated with those of inferior inclinations; those of noble inclinations came together and associated with those of noble inclinations.

In the future too, |bhikkhus,::::| beings will come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations will come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations; those of noble inclinations will come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.

Even now, |bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate by way of the elements. Those of inferior inclinations come together and associate with those of inferior inclinations; those of noble inclinations come together and associate with those of noble inclinations.”
