---
title: Appassutamūlaka sutta - Beginning With Little Learning
description: Beginning with those of little learning, the Buddha shows how the uneducated and undiscerning naturally coalesce with the lazy and forgetful, while the learned and energetic band together.
qualities: learning, laziness, rousing of energy, muddle-mindedness, mindfulness, lack of discernment, wisdom
theme: principle, cultivating discernment, wisdom
slug: sn14.21
---

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. Those with |little learning::ignorant, uneducated [appassuta]| come together and associate with those with little learning; the |lazy::procrastinating, inactive person, indolent [kusīta]| come together and associate with the lazy; the |undiscerning::without wisdom; lit. bad understanding [duppañña]| come together and associate with the undiscerning; the |very learned::well educated, knowledgeable [bahussuta]| come together and associate with the very learned; those |with energy aroused::with initiative [āraddhavīriya]| come together and associate with those with energy aroused; the |wise::discerning, percipient; lit. having the wisdom quality [paññavant]| come together and associate with the wise.

Those with little learning come together and associate with those with little learning; the |muddle-minded::forgetful, not mindful [muṭṭhassatī]| come together and associate with the muddle-minded; the undiscerning come together and associate with the undiscerning; the very learned come together and associate with the very learned; those |attending mindfully::with presence of mind [upaṭṭhitassatī]| come together and associate with those attending mindfully; the wise come together and associate with the wise.”
