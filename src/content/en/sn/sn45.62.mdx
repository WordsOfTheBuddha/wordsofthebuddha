title: Dutiya yonisomanasikārasampadā sutta - Accomplishment in Radical Attention (Second)
description: The Buddha teaches that accomplishment in radical attention naturally leads to the cultivation and frequent practice of the noble eightfold path, culminating in the dispelling of passion, aversion, and delusion.
qualities: radical attention, cultivation, passion, aversion, delusion
theme: wisdom, inspiration
slug: sn45.62
---

“That is—accomplishment in radical attention. For a bhikkhu accomplished in radical attention, |bhikkhus,::::| this is to be expected: they will cultivate the noble eightfold path, they will frequently practice the noble eightfold path.

And how|, bhikkhus,::::| does a bhikkhu accomplished in radical attention cultivate the noble eightfold path and frequently practice the noble eightfold path? Here, |bhikkhus,::::| a bhikkhu cultivates right view, which culminates in the dispelling of |passion::intense desire, strong emotion, infatuation, obsession, lust [rāga]|, culminates in the dispelling of |aversion::hatred, hostility, mental attitude of rejection, fault-finding, resentful disapproval [dosa]|, and culminates in the dispelling of |delusion::illusion, misperception, erroneous belief, false idea, misapprehension; a fundamental distortion of reality that sustains confusion, clouds discernment, and fuels further doubt [moha]| ... cultivates right collectedness, which culminates in the dispelling of passion, culminates in the dispelling of aversion, and culminates in the dispelling of delusion. It is in this way|, bhikkhus,::::| that a bhikkhu accomplished in radical attention cultivates the noble eightfold path and frequently practices the noble eightfold path.”
