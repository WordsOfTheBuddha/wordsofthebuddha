---
title: Pātāla sutta - Abyss
description: The Buddha reveals that the true abyss is painful bodily feeling. Where untrained minds drown in despair, the disciple of the Noble Ones endures life-threatening pain, standing firm and finding solid ground.
qualities: felt experience, sorrow, lamentation, patience, delusion
theme: wisdom, cultivating discernment, inspiration
slug: sn36.4
simile: abyss
priority: 1.2
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| an |uninstructed::uninitiated, untaught, untrained [assutavant]| ordinary person says: ‘There is an |abyss::bottomless pit, netherworld [pātāla]| in the great ocean.’ But, |bhikkhus,::::| when the uninstructed ordinary person speaks thus: ‘There is an abyss in the great ocean,’ they are speaking of something that does not exist and is not found.

This|, bhikkhus,::::| is rather a designation for painful bodily |feelings::pleasant, neutral, or painful sensation, the experience felt on contact through eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind; second of the five aggregates [vedanā]|, namely, ‘the abyss.’

|Bhikkhus,::::| an uninstructed ordinary person, when touched by a painful bodily feeling, grieves, grows weary, laments, weeps beating their breast, and experiences |delusion::bewilderment, confusion [sammoha]|. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is called ‘an uninstructed ordinary person who has not |stood firm in::alighted on; lit. stood back up [paccuṭṭhāsi]| the abyss, who has not found a |footing::firm ground, support [gādha]|.’

But a learned disciple of the Noble Ones, |bhikkhus,::::| when touched by a painful bodily feeling, does not grieve, does not grow weary, does not lament, does not weep beating their breast, and does not experience delusion. This|, bhikkhus,::::| is called ‘a learned disciple of the Noble Ones who has stood firm in the abyss, who has found a footing.’

Whoever does not |endure::tolerate, weather, bear [adhivāseti]| these
arisen painful feelings,
bodily and |life-threatening::fatal; lit. taking life [pāṇahara]|;
who |trembles::shakes, is agitated [pavedhati]| when touched by them;

A weakling of little strength,
who wails and weeps;
he has not stood firm in the abyss,
nor has he found a footing therein.

But whoever endures these
arisen painful feelings,
bodily and life-threatening,
who does not tremble when touched by them;
he indeed has stood firm in the abyss,
and has found a footing.”
