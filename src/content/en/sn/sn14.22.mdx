title: Kusītamūlaka sutta - Beginning With Laziness
description: The Buddha explains how laziness draws unmindful companions, while aroused energy, mindfulness, and discernment form noble bonds.
qualities: laziness, rousing of energy, muddle-mindedness, mindfulness, lack of discernment, wisdom

At Sāvatthi.

“|Bhikkhus,::::| beings come together and associate |by way of the elements::according to disposition; as per character; lit. from state [dhātuso]|. The |lazy::procrastinating, inactive person, indolent [kusīta]| come together and associate with the lazy; the |muddle-minded::forgetful, not mindful [muṭṭhassatī]| come together and associate with the muddle-minded; the |undiscerning::without wisdom; lit. bad understanding [duppañña]| come together and associate with the undiscerning; those |with energy aroused::with initiative [āraddhavīriya]| come together and associate with those with energy aroused; those |attending mindfully::with presence of mind [upaṭṭhitassatī]| come together and associate with those attending mindfully; the |wise::discerning, percipient; lit. having the wisdom quality [paññavant]| come together and associate with the wise.”
