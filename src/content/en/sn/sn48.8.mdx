---
title: Daṭṭhabba sutta - To Be Seen
description: The Buddha explains exactly where the five spiritual faculties are to be seen in practice. Faith is to be seen in the factors of stream-entry, energy in the four right strivings, mindfulness in the four establishments of mindfulness, collectedness in the four jhānas, and wisdom in the Four Noble Truths.
qualities: faith, energy, mindfulness, collectedness, wisdom
theme: wisdom, principle
priority: 1.1
slug: sn48.8
---

“|Bhikkhus,::::| there are these five faculties. What five? The |faculty of faith::mental faculty of confidence, conviction, trust [saddhindriya]|, the |faculty of energy::mental faculty of willpower, determination [vīriya]|, the |faculty of mindfulness::mental faculty of awareness, presence [satindriya]|, the |faculty of collectedness::mental faculty of stability of mind, mental composure [samādhindriya]|, and the |faculty of wisdom::mental faculty of discernment [paññindriya]|.

And where|, bhikkhus,::::| is the faculty of faith to be seen? In the four factors of stream-entry—here the faculty of faith is to be seen.

And where|, bhikkhus,::::| is the faculty of energy to be seen? In the four right strivings—here the faculty of energy is to be seen.

And where|, bhikkhus,::::| is the faculty of mindfulness to be seen? In the four establishments of mindfulness—here the faculty of mindfulness is to be seen.

And where|, bhikkhus,::::| is the faculty of collectedness to be seen? In the four jhānas—here the faculty of collectedness is to be seen.

And where|, bhikkhus,::::| is the faculty of wisdom to be seen? In the four noble truths—here the faculty of wisdom is to be seen.

These|, bhikkhus,::::| are the five faculties.”
